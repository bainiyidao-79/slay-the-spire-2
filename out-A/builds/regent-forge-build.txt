<h2>Forge — the Sovereign Blade One-Shot</h2>
<p><strong>Play pattern:</strong> play Forge cards to grow the Sovereign Blade (base 10 damage, cost 2, Retain) into a boss-killing sword, then one-shot something. Fair warning from every source: this is the archetype to avoid as a plan A. The blade rarely gets replayed in a combat, some bosses shut it down, and the rate is poor — Spoils of Battle spends a whole card to make the blade a 20-damage attack, while a plain common does 18 damage unupgraded by itself.</p>

<p><strong>Relics:</strong> <strong>Electric Shrymp</strong>, an ancient relic, is the one named combo piece — it combos with The Smith to get the biggest Forge card into play.</p>
<p><strong>How to pilot it:</strong> only move into Forge if Seeking Edge shows up; otherwise ignore Forge cards unless you&#39;re desperate. Treat Bulwark as your Block plan, not just a Forge enabler. Expect it to feel slow.</p>
<p><strong>Strengths and weaknesses:</strong> spectacular one-shot kills and a unique playstyle; but it&#39;s the least reliable build here — slow, easily hosed by certain bosses, and out-scaled by the character&#39;s own commons.</p>
