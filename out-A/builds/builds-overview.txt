<h2>Four Rules That Apply to Every Build</h2>
<p>These come straight from the community deckbuilding guides, and they survive every patch so far:</p>
<p><strong>1. Let the deck build itself — don&#39;t force a plan on floor one.</strong> Pick strong, flexible cards early; the archetype you commit to should be the one the run offers you, not the one you queued up wanting.</p>
<p><strong>2. Learn your character&#39;s archetype list before you draft.</strong> The difference between a good deck and a pile is knowing, for example, that Regent&#39;s Star Engine wants Glow/Big Bang/Royal Gamble while Sovereign Blade wants Forge cards — so a &quot;good card&quot; from the wrong archetype is a trap.</p>
<p><strong>3. Don&#39;t make your deck too thin — but do thin it.</strong> Engine characters (Regent, Defect) want small, focused decks — the Regent guide works in the 10–15 card range with dedicated deck-thinning cards (Begone, Charge) that turn dead strikes and curses into fuel. The rule isn&#39;t &quot;small deck,&quot; it&#39;s &quot;no dead cards.&quot;</p>
<p><strong>4. It gets easier — Act 1 is the hardest part.</strong> Your starting deck is the worst it will ever be on floor one; early picks should solve the immediate act (AoE for four-enemy elite fights, one solid block plan), not your late-game fantasy.</p>
