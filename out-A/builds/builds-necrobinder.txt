19:T210e,<p>The Necrobinder runs two proven archetypes in Slay the Spire 2: the <strong>Doom Deck</strong>, which marks enemies for execution, and the <strong>Osty Deck</strong>, which turns her skeletal hand companion into an unkillable front line. Both are viable from Act 1 — pick Doom if you like delayed burst kills, pick Osty if you want to play a near-unkillable tank.</p>
<h2>How the Necrobinder Plays</h2>
<p>The Necrobinder is one of the two new characters in Slay the Spire 2, alongside the Regent — a &quot;sassy lich&quot; from the Spire&#39;s forgotten history. She is a fragile but tactically dense spellcaster who works as a <strong>duo-unit</strong>: you manage both her own limited resources and her companion <strong>Osty</strong>, a massive reanimated skeletal hand that does the dirty work. Her starting relic, <strong>Osty&#39;s Binding</strong>, is what summons Osty in the first place.</p>
<p>Her strengths, per the Mobalytics character guide:</p>
<ul>
<li>Can easily manipulate the Draw/Discard pile</li>
<li>Excellent tank potential with Osty</li>
<li>Insane card draw via Souls</li>
</ul>
<p>And her weaknesses:</p>
<ul>
<li>Requires high synergy — she punishes unfocused decks</li>
<li>A slower playstyle that can be tricky to pilot</li>
</ul>
<p>Those strengths and weaknesses explain both builds below: each one commits to a specific synergy package rather than mixing everything.</p>
<h2>Build 1: The Doom Deck (Execute Archetype)</h2>
<p>Doom is a unique &quot;death-mark&quot; mechanic: enemies whose HP is at or below their <strong>Doom</strong> stacks are executed at the end of their own turn. The payoff is noticeably higher number values on your cards; the cost is that you must survive until the clock ticks down, so a reliable <strong>Block</strong> plan is part of the deck, not an afterthought.</p>

<h3>Relics to Watch</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Book Repair Knife</strong> — a very reliable source of healing, which matters in a deck that tanks while Doom ticks down.</li>
<li><strong>Undying Sigil</strong> — greatly helps weaken enemies on their final turn alive.</li>
</ul>
<h3>How to Pilot It</h3>
<ol>
<li>Take your early Doom cards and one or two Block tools; don&#39;t greedy-pick payoffs before you have the marks to trigger them.</li>
<li>Keep a reliable Block plan — delayed damage is the archetype&#39;s big drawback, so avoid tanking unnecessary hits.</li>
<li>Splash a small <strong>Soul package</strong> for card draw. This helps you find your key Doom cards faster and more often instead of relying on the mediocre ones.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Build 2: The Osty Deck (Summon Tank Archetype)</h2>
<p>The Necrobinder starts with a significantly lower HP pool than other characters. The Osty Deck turns that weakness into the build&#39;s engine: by using the <strong>Summon</strong> mechanic efficiently you bolster Osty, effectively stacking an endless supply of health — and because Osty&#39;s health is not Block, <strong>it doesn&#39;t wear off</strong> between turns.</p>

<h3>Relic to Watch</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Bone Flute</strong> — it may seem small, but every bit of Block can make all the difference in a build that lives and dies by Osty&#39;s survival.</li>
</ul>
<h3>How to Pilot It</h3>
<ol>
<li>The build above focuses on <strong>growing and keeping Osty alive</strong> — protect the hand, and it protects you.</li>
<li>There is also a more <strong>sacrificial variant</strong> built around cards like Bone Shards. Both are legitimate; just be careful not to mix and match the wrong cards, because the two plans want different Osty states.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Meta Cross-Check: Current S-Tier Necrobinder Cards</h2>
<p>The Mobalytics card tier list (checked September 2026) puts the following Necrobinder cards in <strong>S tier</strong>: Demesne, Friendship, Neurosurge, Call of the Void, Sleight of Flesh, Putrefy, Danse Macabre, Lethality, Delay, Reanimate, Enfeebling Touch, Defy, and Reaper Form. Two of them — <strong>Delay</strong> and <strong>Reanimate</strong> — also appear in the Osty build&#39;s high-synergy list above, which is a good sign the deck-building advice and the current meta agree.</p>
<p>If you want the full card-by-card reasoning, see our <a href="/guide/tier-list">Slay the Spire 2 Tier List</a> page, which breaks down cards by tier and updates with each patch.</p>
<h2>Which Build Should You Pick?</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Pick Doom</strong> if you enjoy planning a kill two turns in advance and don&#39;t mind a slower, more deliberate pace. Its AoE marks (Deathbringer, Negative Pulse) also handle multi-enemy fights cleanly.</li>
<li><strong>Pick Osty</strong> if you want the safest route through Act 1–2: an oversized hand absorbing hits forever while your cheap attacks (Snap, Fetch, Rattle) pile up damage.</li>
<li><strong>Commit to one.</strong> The Necrobinder&#39;s weakness list says it plainly: she requires high synergy. Half-Doom, half-Osty decks get the drawbacks of both plans and the payoffs of neither.</li>
</ul>
<p>Both archetypes are sensitive to balance patches — when a new version lands, check the <a href="/news/patch-notes">patch notes page</a> for what changed before you lock in a run.</p>
