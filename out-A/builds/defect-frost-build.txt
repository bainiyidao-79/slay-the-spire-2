<h2>Frost Tank (Orb Slots + Focus)</h2>
<p><strong>Play pattern:</strong> Channel a wall of Frost Orbs, add Orb slots and Focus, and become unkillable while your Orbs grind the enemy down. Frost is called the strongest Orb type — Block is the most important resource, and Frost generation that blocks forever without spending Energy is &quot;crazy&quot;.</p>

<p><strong>Relics:</strong> Runic Capacitor (great with lots of Frost), Gold-Plated Cables, Emotion Chip, and Data Disk (Focus is always welcome).</p>
<p><strong>How to pilot it:</strong> Loop discipline is the skill test — keep a Frost Orb at the front and don&#39;t spam, because every Channel pushes it forward and Evokes it. Add slots, Focus, Hailstorm and Coolant, and &quot;you become unkillable.&quot;</p>
<p><strong>Strengths and weaknesses:</strong> the safest route through Acts 2–3, immune to draw variance; like all Orb plans it lacks burst, so long fights lean on passives.</p>
