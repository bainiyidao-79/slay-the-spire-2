17:T920,<p>The Dominate engine is the Ironclad&#39;s most consistent scaling build in the current meta: turn Vulnerability into Strength with <strong>Dominate</strong>, then let <strong>Bully</strong>, <strong>Uppercut</strong> and your Strikes cash it in.</p>
<h2>The Dominate Engine (Vulnerability → Strength)</h2>
<p>The plan: apply Vulnerability, then convert it. <strong>Dominate</strong> gives you one Strength for each Vulnerability on the enemy <em>plus</em> an immediate Vulnerability — one card that adds both flat damage and multiplies your total damage. Multiple copies scale with each other: more Vuln application means more Strength means bigger hits that apply more Vuln.</p>

Once two or more Vulnerability stacks are on the target, <strong>Bully</strong> — a zero-cost Attack that scales its damage with the target&#39;s Vulnerability count — starts hitting like a premium card.</p>
