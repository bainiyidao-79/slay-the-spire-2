19:Tff7,<p>The community guide calls <strong>Sly</strong> probably the most powerful mechanic on any character — this discard-fueled route is the Silent&#39;s best-by-far build.</p>
<h2>The Sly Discard Engine (Best Route)</h2>
<p><strong>Play pattern:</strong> stack discard enablers and Sly payoffs until &quot;discard a card&quot; means &quot;play a card for free.&quot; The video guide is blunt: &quot;right now Sly is just so good that there&#39;s almost no reason to do anything else.&quot;</p>

<p><strong>Relics:</strong> Ring of the Snake (the extra turn-one draw feeds Backstab and the whole discard plan) and Shuriken, whose Strength makes free Ricochet plays snowball.</p>
<p><strong>How to pilot it:</strong> Tactician (Energy when discarded) still works — upgraded especially — but is no longer necessary: discarding a Sly card and getting the play for free is worth as much as the Energy. Momentum Mori is fine through early Act 2, after which free Sly plays beat spending Energy. For the kill itself, &quot;you kind of just kill with whatever&quot; — Corrosive Wave, the Sly payoffs, plus After Image and Serpent Form as extra win conditions.</p>
<p><strong>Strengths and weaknesses:</strong> the strongest plan on arguably the current best character; the only real failure mode is lacking discard enablers early — Haze in particular is a bad card in Act 1 without them.</p>
