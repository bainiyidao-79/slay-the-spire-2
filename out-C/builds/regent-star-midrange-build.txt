<h2>Star Mid-Range (&quot;Star Good Stuff&quot;)</h2>
<p><strong>Play pattern:</strong> don&#39;t combo — just play the best Star cards and win on rate. Many Star payoffs cost no Energy, so your Energy goes to Block while zero-cost payoffs handle offense. <strong>Void Form</strong> supercharges everything: the first two cards you play each turn are free, <em>including their Star costs</em>.</p>

<p><strong>Relics:</strong> again Crown of Stars carries the plan — its opening Stars are what make turn-one payoffs like Astral Pulse real.</p>
<p><strong>How to pilot it:</strong> the archetypes overlap heavily, so take quality cards first and choose a direction later — Convergence, Glow, Hidden Cache and Genesis fit roughly 80% of Regent decks. Solar Strike (8 damage, gain a Star) and Gather Light are solid role players that open future picks.</p>
<p><strong>Strengths and weaknesses:</strong> forgiving and flexible, with great Act 2–3 tempo; it depends on hitting the character&#39;s high-end rares, so skip aggressively — the bimodal pool punishes mediocre picks.</p>
