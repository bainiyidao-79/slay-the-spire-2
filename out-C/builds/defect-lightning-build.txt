17:Td43,<p>The Lightning aggro route — <strong>Ball Lightning</strong>, <strong>Thunder</strong> and <strong>Storm</strong> clear acts before the enemies set up.</p>
<h2>Big Zap (Lightning Aggro)</h2>
<p><strong>Play pattern:</strong> the most offensive playstyle — flood Lightning, double it with Powers, and Evoke everything. Storm Channels Lightning whenever you play a Power (upgrade: 2; two Storms means 4 per Power), Thunder&#39;s Evoke hits for 12 and effectively doubles your Lightning damage, and Voltaic instantly Evokes excess Orbs as the finisher.</p>

<p><strong>Relics:</strong> Metronome is the standout — this deck Channels constantly, making it &quot;free AoE 30 damage&quot; — plus Emotion Chip.</p>
<p><strong>How to pilot it:</strong> take Orb-fillers (Ball Lightning, Lightning Rod) before payoffs like Barrage; stack Storm and Thunder; treat Hyper Beam as a finisher, not an opener, unless your deck ignores Orbs.</p>
<p><strong>Strengths and weaknesses:</strong> the highest tempo in the kit; shares the weaker burst ceiling, and spamming Orbs at the wrong time wastes payoffs.</p>
