<h2>Claw — Zero-Cost Spam</h2>
<p><strong>Play pattern:</strong> the returning fan favorite — a spammy deck of 0-cost attacks that cycle and buff each other, led by Claw, which permanently strengthens every Claw in your deck each time one is played. Mobalytics calls it devastating; the video is blunter: Claw alone is rated D-tier in StS2 — you&#39;d need 16–17 plays for ~300 cumulative damage against late bosses — so treat this as the fun pick and point All for One at <em>good</em> zero-cost cards to win with the same shell.</p>

<p><strong>How to pilot it:</strong> two warnings from the material: don&#39;t take every 0-cost card you see (deck bloat makes finding Claw harder), and since the deck ignores Orbs, stack some Frost as passive Block anyway.</p>
<p><strong>Strengths and weaknesses:</strong> iconic, fast and fun, but with a far lower damage ceiling than the Orb plans — lean on the All for One + good-zero-cost-cards version.</p>
