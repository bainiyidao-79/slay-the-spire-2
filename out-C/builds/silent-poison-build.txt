<h2>Poison — Inevitability</h2>
<p><strong>Play pattern:</strong> &quot;you&#39;re really just a block deck.&quot; Poison needs no synergy to kill — one well-timed source scales to 450+ damage if the fight runs long — so the entire build question is how long you can survive. Priority one is defense: Footwork, Abrasive, Leg Sweep, Shadow Meld and Well-Laid Plans come before any Poison card.</p>

<p><strong>Relics:</strong> the material recommends no specific relics for Poison — the build lives or dies on Block cards, not relics.</p>
<p><strong>How to pilot it:</strong> survive first, poison second. Block with Footwork-stacked Dexterity, drop Noxious Fumes, and let the triangle numbers work; supplement with Burst lines. The fight <em>should</em> go long — that&#39;s the plan, not a problem.</p>
<p><strong>Strengths and weaknesses:</strong> the most reliable scaling in the game for long fights, and it asks nothing of your deck&#39;s other cards; it&#39;s simply slower than Sly, which is why the video ranks it third without calling it bad.</p>
