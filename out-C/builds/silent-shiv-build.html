Shiv Build — Slay the Spire 2 Build Guide

Shiv Spam build for the Silent in Slay the Spire 2: core cards, relics and step-by-step strategy.

Fill your hand with zero-cost blades and multiply them — the classic Silent aggro route, powered by Accuracy and Hidden Daggers.

Shiv Spam

Play pattern: flood cheap attacks and multiply them. The caveat up front: this build is somewhat rare-dependent — "if you don't get Fan of Knives, it's kind of hard," but with the rares it's excellent.

Core cards:

Card Cost Effect Why it earns a slot
Up My Sleeve — Shiv generation whose cost decreases until it's 0 "Probably the best Shiv card" — what Blade Dance used to be
Leading Strike 1 11 damage at worst; beta version makes two Shivs A "permanent Blade Dance" that bridges you into the archetype
Blade of Ink — Beta version makes three Inky Shivs "Kind of just a Blade Dance with upside... a really good card now"
Blade Dance 1 12 damage in Shivs; now Exhausts Low risk — it removes itself, so it barely counts against your deck size
Cloak and Dagger — Shivs plus Block Only take it upgraded
Accuracy — Shivs deal more damage The classic Shiv payoff
Fan of Knives — Makes your Shivs hit all enemies The rare that makes or breaks the build
Knife Trap — Shiv payoff that grows with every Shiv played "Probably the best Shiv payoff" — the card the video is most excited to open; dead weight before you have Shivs
Serpent Form — Power that boosts your Shivs (4 → 6 per the video) Take it even before the payoffs — "it kind of just solves every fight"
Strangulate — Heavy single-target damage Excellent in Act 1; falls off by Act 3
Finisher — Damage per attack played this turn Fine, but Knife Trap, Envenom and Serpent Form do the job proactively

Relics: Shuriken and Kunai — on this character they're run-defining, converting every spam turn into permanent Strength/Dexterity.

How to pilot it: draft the entry generators first (Up My Sleeve, Leading Strike, Blade of Ink, Blade Dance), and only then the payoffs — Accuracy and Knife Trap do literally nothing without Shivs in the deck.

Strengths and weaknesses: explosive multi-card turns and the best AoE route on the character; heavily reliant on the right rares appearing.
