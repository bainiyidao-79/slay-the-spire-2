19:Te36,<p>Slay the Spire 2 is the sequel to Mega Crit&#39;s genre-defining roguelike deckbuilder. It launched into <strong>Steam Early Access on March 5, 2026 at $24.99</strong>, developed and published by Mega Crit, and is playable on <strong>PC (Windows, macOS and Linux)</strong> with <strong>Steam Deck Verified</strong> status. This page is the fast overview; the build- and patch-level detail lives in the guide sections.</p>
<h2>What&#39;s New Compared to the First Game</h2>
<p>Slay the Spire 2 keeps the core loop — climb a spire, draft a deck, fight turn-based card battles — and adds a set of genuinely new systems:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Four-player online co-op.</strong> The flagship new feature: the first game in the series with online co-op, and all five characters are playable in it. See our <a href="/game-overview/co-op-and-multiplayer">Co-op &amp; Multiplayer page</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Two new characters.</strong> Alongside returning favorites Ironclad, Silent and Defect, Early Access debuts the <strong>Regent</strong> (a star-forging sovereign) and the <strong>Necrobinder</strong> (a lich who fights alongside a giant skeletal hand named Osty).</li>
<li><strong>New mechanics per character.</strong> Doom (a death-mark that executes enemies), Stars (a second resource pool that carries over between turns), Forge (a signature weapon you buff all run) and Summon all change how deckbuilding works.</li>
<li><strong>Enchantments.</strong> Cards can carry enchantments — effects that sit at the bottom of the card and trigger under conditions like &quot;first time you play this card,&quot; which opens combo lines that did not exist in the first game.</li>
<li><strong>Branching Act variants.</strong> Acts come in variants such as Underdocks and Overgrowth, with different elites, events and bosses, so two runs feel less alike.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Early Access Status</h2>
<p>The game is <strong>content-complete enough to review and play for hundreds of hours, but still growing</strong>. Mega Crit ships <strong>beta patches roughly every two weeks</strong>, mixing balance changes, new art and UI work; the beta branch periodically graduates to the main branch. What that means for you:</p>
<ul>
<li>Balance moves patch to patch — check the <a href="/news/patch-notes">Patch Notes page</a> before locking in a build.</li>
<li>Some systems (console versions, the full 1.0 scope) are not finalized yet — see <a href="/game-overview/release-date-and-platforms">Release Date &amp; Platforms</a>.</li>
</ul>
<h2>The Reception, Honestly</h2>
<p>Slay the Spire 2 is simultaneously one of the most-played games of its launch window and a &quot;Mixed&quot; Steam review — the result of two review-bomb waves around balance and communication, not a dead game. The numbers and the full story are on the <a href="/game-overview/reviews-and-reception">Reviews &amp; Reception page</a>.</p>
