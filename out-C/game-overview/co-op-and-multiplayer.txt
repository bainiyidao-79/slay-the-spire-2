17:Td37,<p>Slay the Spire 2 is the first game in the series with multiplayer: <strong>online co-op for up to 4 players</strong>, and all five characters are playable in it. It&#39;s the flagship new feature of the sequel. Here&#39;s the state of it in Early Access.</p>
<h2>The Basics</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>4-player online co-op</strong> runs through the full spire climb — the flagship feature Mega Crit built the sequel around.</li>
<li>All five characters (Ironclad, Silent, Defect, Regent, Necrobinder) are co-op playable, and several kits have co-op-flavored support — for example, the v0.111.0 patch explicitly buffed <strong>The Boot</strong> relic so its damage bonus now also applies to <strong>Osty&#39;s attacks</strong>, the Necrobinder companion that shadows the party.</li>
<li>There&#39;s a small <strong>multiplayer-only card pool</strong> — support cards you use on allies. The current standings from the community card table: <strong>Knockdown, Flanking, Beacon of Hope, Rally, Glimpse Beyond, Energy Surge and Huddle Up</strong> in S tier; <strong>Demonic Shield</strong> mid-table; and <strong>Tank</strong> — a card that makes you take double damage so allies take half — close to universally panned near the bottom.</li>
</ul>
<h2>How Balanced Is It?</h2>
<p>Honestly: it&#39;s fun, and it&#39;s still being tuned. The most-upvoted community thread on the subject right now is literally titled <em>&quot;Slay the Spire 2 multiplayer is fun but seriously unbalanced&quot;</em> (r/slaythespire, September 2026) — which matches what the beta patch cadence suggests: Mega Crit is shipping balance changes every two weeks and co-op is a big part of what they&#39;re iterating on. Expect the meta here to move faster than single-player.</p>
<h2>Crossplay — Not Yet Answerable</h2>
<p>The game is <strong>PC-only in Early Access</strong> (Steam, Windows/macOS/Linux — see <a href="/game-overview/release-date-and-platforms">Release Date &amp; Platforms</a>). That makes the &quot;can Steam players queue with console players?&quot; question moot until console versions exist, and <strong>whether PC–console crossplay will be supported has not been announced</strong>. When console dates land, this section updates with the real answer.</p>
<h2>Local Co-op</h2>
<p>No couch / split-screen co-op mode is part of Early Access, and none has been announced. If you want to play together in one room, you&#39;re looking at online play from separate machines (or Steam Remote Play, which is not the same as native local co-op — treat that as untested until Mega Crit comments on it).</p>
<h2>Quick Answers</h2>
<p><strong>Is Slay the Spire 2 multiplayer?</strong> Yes — 4-player online co-op, the series&#39; first.</p>
<p><strong>Is there crossplay?</strong> Not applicable yet (PC-only), and unannounced for the future.</p>
<p><strong>Is there local co-op?</strong> Not in Early Access, and not announced.</p>
<p><strong>Do all characters work in co-op?</strong> Yes — all five, plus a small pool of multiplayer-only support cards.</p>
