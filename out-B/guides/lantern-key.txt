19:T1246,<p>The <strong>Lantern Key</strong> in Slay the Spire 2 is a quest card from a combat event of the same name: you find a faintly glowing key in <strong>the Hive</strong>, choose to fight a Mysterious Knight for it instead of taking 100 gold, and the unplayable Lantern Key card then unlocks a special event — <strong>War Historian, Repy</strong> — in the next Act (Act 3).</p>
<h2>What the Lantern Key Is</h2>
<p>The Lantern Key appears as an <strong>Event</strong> found exclusively in the Hive. It&#39;s a combat-room-style event: you come upon the glowing key and pick it up, and its owner — a person who &quot;looks like bad news&quot; — immediately shows up: <em>&quot;Been looking all over for that key! Now if you don&#39;t mind...&quot;</em></p>
<p>The key itself travels with you as the <strong>Lantern Key card</strong>, an <strong>unplayable</strong> card with a quest marker whose text reads: <em>&quot;Unlocks a special event in the next Act.&quot;</em> It is not a relic and not something you play — it just sits in your deck as a pass to the Act 3 encounter.</p>
<h2>How to Get the Lantern Key</h2>
<p>At the event you have two choices:</p>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Option</th>
<th>Result</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody><tr>
<td><strong>Return the Key</strong></td>
<td>The stranger thanks you and rewards you handsomely: <strong>100 Gold</strong>.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Keep the Key</strong></td>
<td>The stranger turns menacing — &quot;it seems like only one of you is making it out of here with the key&quot; — and you enter combat against the <strong>Mysterious Knight</strong> for the Lantern Key card.</td>
</tr>
</tbody></table>
<p>Win the fight and the Lantern Key card is yours. The <strong>Mysterious Knight</strong> is a stronger version of the Flail Knight (from the Knight Gang encounter) with innate <strong>Strength</strong> and <strong>Plating</strong>. Its HP isn&#39;t listed in our sources (<strong>to be confirmed</strong>), but its move pattern is documented:</p>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Move</th>
<th>Effect</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody><tr>
<td><strong>Ram</strong> (opening move)</td>
<td>Deals 15 damage (17 on Ascension 9+)</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Flail</strong></td>
<td>Deals 9 damage x2 (10 x2 on Ascension 9+)</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Breaker</strong></td>
<td>Gains 3 Strength</td>
</tr>
</tbody></table>
<p>After the opening Ram, it randomly picks Breaker (equal chance), Flail (double chance), or Ram (double chance), and never uses the same move twice in a row — so expect an early Ramp-into-Flail burst and plan your block for the second turn.</p>
<h2>How to Use the Lantern Key</h2>
<p>The Lantern Key card is spent in the <strong>War Historian, Repy</strong> event in <strong>Act 3</strong>. Since update v0.77.0, picking up the key <strong>guarantees that the first unknown room you enter in Act 3 is the War Historian, Repy event</strong>, so the pass always connects to its reward rather than leaving you to hunt for the room.</p>
<p>What exactly the event grants in exchange for the key isn&#39;t documented in our sources — <strong>to be confirmed</strong>. What the patch record does show is that the Repy encounter hands out meaningful extras: a <strong>History Course relic</strong> is attached to the Lantern Key event chain (adjusted in v0.59.0 so it no longer replays cards from two turns ago after a turn where you played nothing), and since v0.105.0 <strong>something special happens if you hold multiple Lantern Key cards</strong> — an interaction that deliberately doesn&#39;t apply in multiplayer, where duplicate Lantern Key bonuses from the Repy event are also disabled.</p>
<h2>Which Option Should You Pick?</h2>
<p>The event is a straight trade: <strong>100 Gold now</strong> (enough to matter for an early shop purchase) versus <strong>a guaranteed Act 3 event pass</strong>. Our sources don&#39;t quantify the Repy event&#39;s payoff, so there&#39;s no documented &quot;correct&quot; answer — the choice is whether the immediate gold serves your current run better than an Act 3 hook you can&#39;t price yet.</p>
<p>The event has been polished steadily across Early Access: the Return reward was set to 100 Gold in v0.86.0, the event became combat-room-style in v0.94.0, a typo was fixed in v0.110.0, and a loading hitch on the event was reduced in v0.111.0.</p>
<p>Fighting the Mysterious Knight is a mid-run check, not a wall — for the fights that actually end runs, see our <a href="/bosses/bosses">boss guide</a>, and if you&#39;re deciding whether a 100-gold swing matters for your deck, the <a href="/guides/tips">tips guide</a> covers shop and gold priorities.</p>
