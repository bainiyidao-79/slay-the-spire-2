Slay the Spire 2 Price — $24.99 Early Access, One Edition

Slay the Spire 2 costs $24.99 on Steam Early Access. One edition, no DLC announced, Steam Deck Verified. What the price includes and what's still unknown.

Slay the Spire 2 costs $24.99 as a one-time purchase on Steam, where it has been in Early Access since March 5, 2026. There is currently a single edition — no deluxe, founder's pack or DLC has been announced.

What You Get for $24.99 Right Now

  • The full Early Access build: five characters, four-player online co-op, enchantments, branching act variants and Ascension modifiers.
  • Bi-weekly beta patches — balance changes, new art and UI work ship continuously, and the beta branch graduates to the main branch over time (see Patch Notes).
