<h2>The Osty Deck (Summon Tank Archetype)</h2>
<p>The Necrobinder starts with a significantly lower HP pool than other characters. The Osty Deck turns that weakness into the build&#39;s engine: by using the <strong>Summon</strong> mechanic efficiently you bolster Osty, effectively stacking an endless supply of health — and because Osty&#39;s health is not Block, <strong>it doesn&#39;t wear off</strong> between turns.</p>

<p>As with the Doom list, the first four are early pickups; Fetch through Spur are the high-synergy payoffs that need commitment.</p>
<h3>Relic to Watch</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Bone Flute</strong> — it may seem small, but every bit of Block can make all the difference in a build that lives and dies by Osty&#39;s survival.</li>
</ul>
<h3>How to Pilot It</h3>
<ol>
<li>The build above focuses on <strong>growing and keeping Osty alive</strong> — protect the hand, and it protects you.</li>
<li>There is also a more <strong>sacrificial variant</strong> built around cards like Bone Shards. Both are legitimate; just be careful not to mix and match the wrong cards, because the two plans want different Osty states.</li>
</ol>
