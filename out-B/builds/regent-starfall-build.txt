<h2>Starfall — the Star Nuke</h2>
<p><strong>Play pattern:</strong> stockpile Stars and Vigor across a turn or two, then cash everything in at once — most reliably through <strong>Radiate</strong>, which deals damage to every enemy for each Star you gained this turn (3 damage per Star by default). With enough setup the video shows Radiate going from 3 to 52 damage in a single turn after banking 25 Stars. If you&#39;ve played Magic: The Gathering, it plays like a storm deck: build up, then kill everything in one go. Starfall is the most reliable archetype community play has found — the backup payoff is <strong>Stardust</strong>, a spend-X damage card (cited at 5 damage per hit), weaker in multi-enemy fights.</p>

<p><strong>Relics:</strong> the material&#39;s relic advice for the Regent is thin — cards simply matter more. Crown of Stars (3 Stars at combat start, 6 upgraded) is the engine every Starfall plan uses, and the explicit advice is to push card rewards and shops first, relic slots second.</p>
<p><strong>How to pilot it:</strong> the dream line is Decisions, Decisions into Royal Gamble — roughly 27 Stars in one turn, which kills almost anything with a small damage boost. Stack Vigor patiently (Patter, Terraforming), don&#39;t attack while banking it, and block until the big turn. If Radiate never shows up, <strong>Lunar Blast</strong> is a worse version that only makes sense with Decisions, Decisions (doubled into itself, replaying Monologue/Terraforming for Strength — the video&#39;s best case reached ~600 damage) or as an infinite-deck payoff.</p>
<p><strong>Strengths and weaknesses:</strong> scales to absurd numbers and crushes bosses; but it leans on specific payoffs, and without Radiate/Royal Gamble you need a lot of sustained generation to get there.</p>
