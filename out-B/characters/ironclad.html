Ironclad — Slay the Spire 2 Character Guide

Ironclad in Slay the Spire 2: 80 HP, A tier on the community table. Mechanics, traits, archetypes and the full build guide.

A battle-hardened warrior who trades health for value and out-scales fights. In Slay the Spire 2 his Vulnerability is a resource: Dominate gains Strength per Vulnerability stack, turning old debuff cards into a scaling engine. He also keeps his starting Strikes more than any other character.

Key Facts

Stat Value
Base HP 80
Community tier A-Tier
Traits Strength Scaling, Self-Healing, Exhaust Synergy, High HP Pool
Archetypes Vulnerability→Strength (Dominate engine) · Exhaust/Block with Body Slam
