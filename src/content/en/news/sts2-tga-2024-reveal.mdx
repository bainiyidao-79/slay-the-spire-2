The Game Awards 2024 gave Slay the Spire 2 its biggest stage yet: a brand-new trailer showcasing fresh cards and new characters, with Mega Crit confirming the plan to open **Early Access in 2025**.

The trailer was the first moving look at the sequel's expanded roster in motion — the new and returning classes, the reworked enemies, and the bosses guarding the second climb. By then the engine story was already public: the sequel had traded Unity for open-source **Godot**, a decision the studio tied to player-facing benefits like modding headroom.

The original Slay the Spire (2019) had defined the deckbuilding roguelike with its blend of turn-based combat and card-driven runs, and TGA's audience responded accordingly — the sequel's trailer became one of the show's most-shared indie reveals.

The 2025 window, as with the original announcement, was still a promise rather than a date. The final schedule: a delay to March 2026 ([announced that September](/news/early-access-delayed-to-march-2026)), then the [March 5, 2026 launch](/news/early-access-dated-march-5-2026) — with two of the trailer's teased newcomers, the [Necrobinder](/classes/necrobinder-character-guide) and the [Regent](/classes/regent-character-guide), headlining the roster reveal season that followed.
