Slay the Spire 2 is patched **roughly every two weeks** on a beta branch, and beta patches periodically graduate to the main branch — so the meta moves twice a month, and this page moves with it. Below: the current patch's changes, the recent flashpoints, and what each shift means for your builds.

## The Rhythm

Mega Crit's own framing (v0.107.0 beta notes, June 4, 2026): "Another bi-weekly beta patch is here!" — with some patches explicitly laying "the groundwork for moving the beta branch to the main branch," meaning stability and bug fixes front-loaded before balance swings. The practical takeaway: **check this page every patch cycle before committing a run**, because card values you memorized last month may literally have changed.

## v0.111.0 (Beta) — The Current Balance Pass

**Balance changes:**

| Change | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Juggernaut** buff | Damage 5(7) → **6(8)** — a real buff to a D-tier card; watch it climb the [Tier List](/cards/tier-list) |
| **Monarch's Gaze** buff (Regent) | Energy cost 3(2) → **2(1)** |
| **Reflect** nerf (Regent) | Block 16(20) → **15(20)** — still top-tier, slightly less absurd |
| **Sic 'Em** buff (Necrobinder) | Summon 2(3) → **3(4)** — direct support for the [Osty build](/builds/builds-overview-necrobinder) |
| **The Scythe** buff (Necrobinder) | Damage 3(4) → **4(5)** |
| **The Boot** relic buff | Damage increase now also applies to **Osty's attacks** — co-op relevant |
| **Skulking Colony** buff | +1 Strength on its gain turn at high Ascension |
| **Aeonglass** rework | No longer applies the Ebb debuff; now gains Block on its Ebb move instead of Increasing Intensity |

**Quality of life:** the Gremlin Merc fight now pays out 50% of the gold reward when the Fat Gremlin escapes with stolen gold (previously 0%).

**UI & experience:** run-start errors now show a popup instead of a black screen; a preferred Ascension can be set for the random-character option; **Vakuu** now tells you your visit count accurately; **Pael's Flesh** correctly updates the displayed max Energy; the Necrobinder's **Sacrifice** card previews its Block gain (planning with Dexterity/Frail just got easier); the Wither status preview matches Aeonglass's actual upgrade level.

**Art:** new Epoch art for Devastation, The 12 and The Giant; a Defect power-up animation; new Soul Nexus VFX. Translations updated across languages.

## The v0.107.x Window — The Flashpoint

The **v0.107.0** beta (June 4, 2026) was deliberately small — "mostly focused on stability," laying groundwork for the beta-to-main transition. The surrounding **v0.107.1 "Major Update 2"** cycle is the one that lives in infamy: it's the patch window at the center of the [review-bomb story](/game-overview/reviews-and-reception), where balance nerfs to infinite-combo decks, the Act 3 **Doormaker** fight and enemy tuning collided with a playerbase that felt unheard. The lesson Mega Crit appears to have taken — and the reason subsequent patches read the way they do — is that balance changes need visible communication.

## What Each Kind of Change Means for Your Builds

- **Card buffs to B/C-tier cards** (Juggernaut, Sic 'Em, The Scythe): these move the [Tier List](/cards/tier-list) bottom-up — a buffed archetype payoff can quietly become a build-around.
- **Nerfs to S-tier staples** (Reflect): the engine survives but the margin shrinks — Recount your Block math before you rely on it.
- **Enemy reworks** (Aeonglass): fight-specific guides age faster than card guides; re-check boss pages after enemy-touching patches.
- **Relic changes** (The Boot): cross-character implications — Osty builds got better in co-op *and* single-player.
