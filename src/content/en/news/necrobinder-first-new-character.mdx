October 2025 brought the sequel's first new-character reveal: the **Necrobinder**, a lich "born of the Spire itself" — and, alongside her, the star of the show: **Osty**, the giant undead hand she fights alongside.

The reveal framed the Necrobinder's whole identity around the summon. Osty attacks independently, soaks damage meant for his 66-HP master, and — the twist that made lich players sit up — interacts with the Necrobinder's own spells in both directions: buffed by necromancy, and **Sacrifice-d** by it when the moment demands. The character's kit traded raw health (the lowest in the game) for a renewable meat shield and a death-themed spellbook.

The reveal article walked through her signature cards: summons that stack Osty's health, sacrificial payoffs, and the **Doom** mechanic — a creeping debuff that executes enemies once their stack meets their health bar, ignoring Block entirely. (See our [Doom burst build](/builds/necrobinder-calamity-burst) for how that turned out in practice.)

With the Necrobinder revealed, the sequel's roster math was public: **three returning classes — Ironclad, Silent, Defect — and two newcomers**, the Necrobinder and, soon after, the starry [Regent](/news/regent-character-reveal). The unlock order would chain them: each new class freed by finishing a run with the last.
