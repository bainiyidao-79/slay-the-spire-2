The 0.103.0 beta patch leaned into run-start variety: **three new Ancient-granted relics**, a batch of card reworks, and a tuning pass that eased the difficulty of several elites and bosses. The headline items:

## New Ancient relics (Neow pool)

- **Neow's Bone** — "Upon pickup, gain 2 random Ancient relics. Add 1 random curse to your deck." Power with a price: two free relic slots' worth of value, paid in deck pollution.
- **Potion-Belt Holster** — "Upon pickup, gain 1 potion slot and 2 random potions." A [Potion Belt](/mechanics/potion-relics-guide) starter kit folded into one pickup — instant value for potion-centric plans.
- **Winged Boots** — "When choosing the next floor, you may ignore the current path 3 times." Three free map rewrites per run — routing freedom that compounds with every [map decision](/basics/map-room-types-route-planning).

## Card changes

The pass included reworks across several packages, continuing the beta's pattern of testing structural changes on the branch before they reach the main game — the same pipeline as the [reverted 0.100 nerfs](/news/0-101-patch-reverts-nerfs), now operating with its feedback loop intact.

## Enemy tuning

Several elites and bosses came down a notch in difficulty, formalizing what the launch meta had already decided: a handful of Act 2–3 walls were outscaling the decks that reached them. The specific nerfs reduced their most oppressive openings and scaling turns.

## The pattern this patch establishes

0.103 is the template the cadence promise is settling into: bi-weekly beta drops, experimental relics and reworks on the branch, measurable nerfs to confirmed problem fights, and the F2-feedback loop deciding what graduates. For players, the practical advice stays the same all cycle: opt into the beta branch if you want tomorrow's cards today, and file F2 reports — they demonstrably steer the ship.
