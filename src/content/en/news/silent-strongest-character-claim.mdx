Two days into Early Access, the first credible balance verdict landed — from a credible mouth. **JapaneseExport**, a player claiming top-five world ranking and 10,000+ hours in the original spire, published his assessment of Slay the Spire 2's launch meta, and the headline was a warning: **the Silent was "undeniably the strongest character" — strong to the point of "worrying."**

The reasoning, in his read of the early meta:

- **Discard-synergy cards overperformed.** The Silent's discard package generated too much value per card, letting her cycle key pieces far faster than the draft economy intended.
- **Early damage came too easy.** One reliable damage card — any of several — covered most of the early game, which meant she could **skip card rewards entirely**, banking gold and avoiding deck pollution while other classes were still forced to draft.
- **The floor was too high for too little effort.** Strong classes are supposed to need a plan; launch Silent didn't especially need one.

The community's broader consensus matched the shape of the take — the Silent was the launch's consensus strongest, with the discard package the usual suspect. (Our [Silent builds](/builds/silent-fan-of-blades) show why: the draw engines and free-value packages really are that good.) Subsequent patches took repeated swings at the package, and by the [0.105 meta read](/mechanics/best-class-0-105-tier-list) the class had cooled from "worrying" to merely excellent.
