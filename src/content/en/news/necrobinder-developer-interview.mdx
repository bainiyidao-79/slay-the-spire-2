December 2025's **PC Gaming Show: Most Wanted** carried a Slay the Spire 2 developer interview that doubled as a design seminar on the game's hardest character: the **Necrobinder**, whose summon-and-sacrifice mechanics the team walked through in unusual depth.

The discussion covered the studio's design philosophy first — how the sequel evolves the original's deckbuilding core without losing the read-at-a-glance clarity that made it a classic — before zooming in on the lich. The Necrobinder, the team explained, was the game's high-difficulty expression: a character whose health pool is deliberately the smallest, and whose survivability is outsourced to **Osty**, the skeletal left hand she summons into every fight.

The mechanics on display:

- **Summon stacking** — Osty's health grows with every Summon effect, making him a rechargeable wall.
- **Sacrifice payoffs** — cards that kill Osty on purpose for immediate value, turning the summon into a reusable resource.
- **The Bound Phylactery loop** — Osty's automatic revival each turn, which makes death on your side of the board a schedule rather than a disaster.

For players, the interview's takeaway was a warning wrapped in an invitation: the Necrobinder was designed as the roster's deep end — complex, fragile, and enormously rewarding for players who think in resources rather than health bars.
