Mega Crit's August 2025 "Neow newsletter" gave Early Access watchers their regular pre-launch drip: a look at relics and potions heading into the sequel — and a quiet admission about how much visual love the little stuff was getting.

**Relics keeping their classics.** The relic lineup leaned on familiar silhouettes from the original spire, updated for the new art style. Among the teases: the **shopping coupon** — a relic that's literally a coupon from the in-game discount chain, a joke only regular customers of the shop would get — alongside cozy gear like the **stone furnace humidifier** (guaranteed good sleep, allegedly) and the **golden Joss Paper**, whose paper-burning luck would later become famous among [exhaust-deck](/builds/ironclad-exhaust-enchant-engine) players as a draw engine.

**Potions get a full glow-up.** The bigger reveal was visual: every potion in the sequel received its own bespoke illustration — no more generic bottle swaps — and the rarest potions were promised a distinct, unmistakable look at a glance. In a game where a potion is often a boss fight's margin of victory, reading your inventory at a glance is a genuine gameplay upgrade.

The newsletter was also a schedule signal: monthly-ish communications, each packaging a few reveals on the road to the (then-planned) launch window. The next big beats — the [Necrobinder reveal](/news/necrobinder-first-new-character), the [Ancients system](/news/ancients-blessings-reveal), and eventually the [delay to March 2026](/news/early-access-delayed-to-march-2026) — all followed in this cadence.
