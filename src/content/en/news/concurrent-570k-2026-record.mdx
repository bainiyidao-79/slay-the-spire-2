The launch weekend's final record: **570,000-plus concurrent players** — a figure that placed Slay the Spire 2 in **Steam's all-time top twenty** by peak concurrency, ahead of Call of Duty, Terraria and a wall of household names, and comfortably the biggest PC launch of 2026 to date.

The comparisons ran out of scale. The peak stood at **ten times the original game's all-time record**; it dwarfed same-genre launches; it embarrassed same-day blockbusters; and with GTA6's PC version nowhere in sight, commentators struggled to name a 2026 release positioned to touch it. Twenty-five hundred-plus reviews were landing per day, nearly all positive, on top of the [95% launch status](/news/launch-day-95-percent-positive).

Mega Crit's response to the milestone was as much letter as tweet: the team said it had been blown away by the number of people playing and sharing their love for the game — five-plus years of development meeting this kind of arrival, they wrote, was beyond what they knew how to process.

The all-time context is worth keeping. Steam's peak-concurrency top twenty is mostly free-to-play titans with decade-long lifespans; a paid, single-purchase Early Access deckbuilder crashing the party said something about the franchise's stored goodwill — and about what happens when a genre's founder ships a sequel that [reviewers scored as a masterpiece](/news/ign-nine-review).
