September 12, 2025: Mega Crit announced that **Slay the Spire 2's Early Access launch was moving to March 2026** — a "mysterious Thursday" in spring, replacing the 2025 window set at announcement.

The framing mattered. This was not a troubled production stumbling past a deadline. The original Slay the Spire remains one of Steam's most beloved games ever — **97% positive across 160,000+ reviews**, "Overwhelmingly Positive" certified — and the sequel carried the weight of being the deckbuilding genre's founding franchise. The team's stated reason was creative headroom: the extra months were for polish, not repair.

The delay set the final launch runway in motion:

- **October 2025:** the first new-character reveal, the [Necrobinder](/news/necrobinder-first-new-character)
- **November 2025:** the [Ancients blessing system](/news/ancients-blessings-reveal)
- **December 2025:** the [Regent reveal](/news/regent-character-reveal) and a [developer deep-dive interview](/news/necrobinder-developer-interview)
- **February 2026:** the [March 5 date confirmation](/news/early-access-dated-march-5-2026)

When the date landed, the payoff was immediate — a launch week that [crashed Steam](/news/launch-crashes-steam-servers) and [broke concurrent-player records](/news/concurrent-570k-2026-record). The delay, in hindsight, bought exactly what it promised.
