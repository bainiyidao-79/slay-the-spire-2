One week. **Three million copies.** Mega Crit's first sales report confirmed Slay the Spire 2 had out-earned every game's opening week in its genre's history — with **25 million runs already started** worldwide, and the studio openly admitting it was "beyond thrilled" while noting, in classic Mega Crit fashion, that the journey was just beginning.

The announcement also carried the first post-launch roadmap, and the shape of Early Access's future:

- **Continuous balance updates** — described as ongoing tuning "based on data and player feedback," a phrase that would soon be tested by the [first beta-balance backlash](/news/beta-patch-backlash-march).
- **Regular content cadence** — new cards, relics, and more, delivered in the game's now-standard update rhythm.
- **The Early Access contract** — the structure that made the original a decade-long phenomenon, reapplied: visible iteration, community-driven priorities, and no content paywalls ([no microtransactions either](/news/no-microtransactions-pledge)).

Context for the number: 3 million in a week beat Elden Ring spin-off Nightreign's opening pace and made the spire the fastest-selling roguelike in Steam history — on top of the [570K concurrency record](/news/concurrent-570k-2026-record), the [global #1 seller slot](/news/tops-steam-global-sellers), and a [9/10 from IGN](/news/ign-nine-review).

The third-party estimate that followed stretched even further: [5.3 million copies in the first month](/news/five-million-first-month-estimate).
