Ever wondered how everyone else's runs go? Mega Crit's May newsletter opened the books: **240 million runs played**, **60.5 million wins**, and **179.5 million deaths** — an overall win rate of **25%**, which the studio notes is far healthier than it sounds for a game designed to kill you.

## The win-rate charts, tiered

- **Overall:** 25% — one in four climbs succeeds.
- **Ascension 0:** 16% average — dramatically kinder than the original game's Ascension-0 figure of 9%. The sequel's opening difficulty is tuned meaningfully friendlier.
- **Ascension 10 (current cap):** about 17% — remarkably, *higher* than the base game's rate, and light-years beyond the original's Ascension 20 win rate of roughly 3%.

The reading: the sequel's difficulty curve is flatter. Early Ascensions no longer cliff the win rate the way the original's did, which keeps the ladder climbable for mid-core players while the ceiling still humbles everyone.

## Who everyone is playing

The class pick data answered the eternal question: **Ironclad leads the pick rate at 24%** — the friendly 80-HP knight remains everyone's first love — with the rest of the roster spread behind him. (Where to start yourself: the [class guide](/classes/classes-overview) and the [difficulty ranking](/basics/class-difficulty-ranking).)

The newsletter also teased upcoming development directions, continuing the monthly-communication rhythm that has carried patch context, [mode teases](/news/three-new-modes-teased) and roadmap beats to players since before launch.

Two hundred forty million runs in under three months is also, quietly, the answer to every "is Early Access worth it" thread: the spire has never been more alive.
