May 8's 0.105 beta patch was the one players had been begging for and the one nobody expected in the same update: the **Doormaker is gone**, a brand-new boss took his chair, the **Bestiary** shipped, and Mega Crit formalized its [bi-weekly patch cadence](/mechanics/0-105-patch-overview).

## Doormaker's farewell tour ends

The Act 3 boss had been the community's longest-running mechanical grievance — a fight whose complexity crossed the studio's own comfort threshold, with lingering bugs the team couldn't cleanly fix. Rather than patch a fundamentally tangled encounter, Mega Crit removed it and built a replacement from zero: **Aeonglass**, whose hourglass-flavored fight now guards the end of Act 3. (Our [Aeonglass guide](/mechanics/aeonglass-boss-guide) covers the verified pattern — 512 HP, Withering Presence, Ebb and Eye Lasers.)

## The Bestiary opens

The Compendium gained its monster index — art, animation entries, and an open invitation to poke at it. The patch notes hid an easter egg in plain sight: **press the 1–8 keys** while viewing a monster. (More on what the book tracks: [Bestiary secrets](/mechanics/bestiary-secrets-lore).)

## Everything else

The balance slate was broad — Blade of Ink's [Inky nerf](/mechanics/blade-of-ink-nerf-explained), the [Tesla Coil+ trigger doubling](/mechanics/tesla-coil-change-explained), the [Sword Sage Replay rework](/mechanics/sword-sage-rework-explained), the Pumpkin Candle rework, three new Neow relics, and the [multi-Lantern-Key surprise](/basics/multiple-lantern-keys-secret) for single-player runs.

The cadence announcement tied it together: two-week patches from here on — bigger changes, more polish, and time for players to actually digest each drop.
