## How to read this list

- **Class** shows the character a relic is restricted to; "Any" relics drop for everyone.
- **Ancient** relics are the drawback-free tier offered by Ancients like [Orobas](/mechanics/orobas-ancient-guide) at act starts — many are upgraded versions of existing relics.
- Event relics come from question-mark events; shop relics are merchant stock.
