Getting dumped from co-op runs every twenty minutes is the most reported multiplayer issue in Early Access — and the cause is usually the least satisfying answer in tech support: **Steam's servers are overloaded**. The game's population spikes regularly, and relay stability suffers at peak hours. Here is what actually helps, and what to rule out first.

## First: rule out your own network

Before blaming the servers, clear the local suspects:

- **Go wired.** Wi-Fi (especially 2.4 GHz) is the top cause of "random" disconnects that only look like server problems.
- **Reboot the router** — a stale NAT table drops long-lived game sessions.
- **Close bandwidth hogs:** streaming, downloads and cloud sync on anyone's machine in the house.
- **Check the host first.** If the *host's* connection hiccups, the whole lobby dissolves — the host needs the stable line, not the guests. See [how hosting works](/basics/how-to-join-multiplayer).

## The server-load reality

At peak hours the relay infrastructure genuinely drops parties. The signatures: disconnects cluster in evening peaks, everyone in the lobby drops at once rather than one player stuttering out, and retrying immediately fails the same way. The fixes are unglamorous:

- **Play off-peak.** Moving the session a few hours (or to a weekend morning) dodges most relay congestion.
- **Retry the lobby.** A fresh host migration often lands on a healthier relay.
- **Wait for the patch.** Server capacity has been a recurring item in patch notes; peak-hour stability improves in waves.

## The advanced workaround: LAN-style play

Groups who need stable long sessions sometimes route the game through a **LAN multiplayer mod** plus a tunneling tool (the kind used to play LAN-only games over the internet). It works — direct connections skip the crowded relays entirely — but setup is genuinely technical, and every player needs the same mod set. Only worth it for regular four-player groups who keep losing Sunday nights to the servers.

## If single-player also disconnects

Cloud-save sync errors and login loops are a different beast — check Steam's service status, verify game files, and see the [launch fixes](/tech/wont-launch-fixes). But when the game itself runs fine and only co-op drops: it's the servers, and the spire will still be there in the morning.
