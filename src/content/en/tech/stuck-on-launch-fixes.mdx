The infinite launch screen — Steam says "Running," the game shows a logo, and nothing else ever happens — has three known killers, in order of success rate. Work top to bottom.

<div class="art-ph lead"><img src="/images/sts2-hero-keyart.webp" loading="eager" alt="Slay the Spire 2 key art"><figcaption>Step one fixes it more often than everything else combined.</figcaption></div>

## Step 1: disable Steam shader pre-caching (highest success)

1. Steam → **Settings → Downloads**.
2. Uncheck **Enable Shader Pre-Caching**.
3. Restart Steam, then launch the game.

Pre-caching compiles shaders at startup; when its cache conflicts with a driver update, the launch sequence stalls exactly where you're stuck.

## Step 2: switch the rendering driver

Steam → right-click the game → **Properties → General → Launch Options**, and add one of (test one at a time, keep the winner):

- `--rendering-driver opengl3` (try this first)
- `--rendering-driver vulkan`

Vulkan/OpenGL conflicts after GPU driver updates are the second-most-common cause of a frozen launch. If the game opens windowed-small or flickering, append `-windowed`.

## Step 3: verify game files

Right-click the game → **Properties → Installed Files → Verify integrity of game files**. Let the scan finish completely before launching again.

## Also worth doing

- **Close the Steam in-game overlay:** Properties → General → uncheck the overlay.
- **Exit background interferers:** Discord overlay, GeForce Experience, recorders, antivirus scans — anything that injects into processes.
- **Run as administrator:** right-click the game exe → Compatibility → administrator.
- **Delete the Sentry file:** in the install folder, remove `libsentry.windows.release.x86_64`, then restart Steam.
- **Update your GPU drivers** if none of the above moved the needle.
