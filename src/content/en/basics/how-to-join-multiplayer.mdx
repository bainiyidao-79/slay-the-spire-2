Slay the Spire 2's headline feature is the series' first **online co-op**: up to four players climbing one shared map, each playing their own class, all fighting the same enemies. Here is how hosting and joining works, plus the co-op rules that surprise new parties.

## Before you start

- Every player needs the game on **Steam** (it is a paid Early Access title — no free weekend trick).
- Host and guests must be **Steam friends** to invite directly.
- The host needs a **stable connection for the whole session** — if the host drops, the lobby dissolves.
- Turn off all **mods and trainers** before matchmaking; they break sync.

## Hosting a run

1. Launch the game → main menu → **Multiplayer**.
2. Choose **Host** and pick the standard mode.
3. Select a **save slot** — you can open a fresh run or continue a single-player save; progress in that slot is shared with the lobby.
4. **Invite Friends** and send invites from your Steam friends list.
5. Once everyone has picked a character and readied up → **Start Game**.

## Joining a run

1. Main menu → **Multiplayer** → **Join**.
2. Accept the Steam invite from your host, or find their lobby in the list.
3. Pick your class, ready up, and wait for the host to start.

## Co-op rules that change the game

- **Shared save, shared unlocks.** The lobby plays in the host's slot; characters and unlocks are shared progress.
- **Enemies scale up with party size.** Enemy HP jumps hard with each additional player; their damage mostly stays flat, so fights turn into longer slugfests, not deadlier ones.
- **Separate card rewards, shared relics.** Every player makes their own card picks and browses their own shop; relics are shared, and each player can grab only one when a reward offers duplicates.
- **Simultaneous combat.** Everyone plays cards at once, with effects resolving in play order. A downed player is not a wipe — they revive after the fight.
- **Voted routing.** The party votes on the next map node; majority rules. Argue before the vote, not after.
