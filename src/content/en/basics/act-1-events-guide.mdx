Question-mark nodes are where Slay the Spire 2 runs are quietly won or lost. Act 1's event pool is full of cheap upgrades — a free pet, a card upgrade, a combat you cannot lose anything in — and it also contains the single best card-removal opportunity in the game. Here is every early event worth knowing, and what to pick.

## Act 1 events that are (almost) always worth it

### Byrdonis Nest

- **Incubate the egg at your campsite** → you gain a pet plus a 0-cost attack card. The attack is a genuine early-game workhorse and the pet is free value. This is the default pick.
- **Eat it** → a small heal. You ate a potential companion. Don't.

### Sapphire Seed

- **Eat it** → heal and randomly upgrade one card in your deck. Reliable, immediate, good.
- **Plant it** → a delayed payoff from a later event, less consistent. Take the sure thing in Act 1.

### The Sunken Statue

- **Pull the sword** → you get a stone-bound sword that grows into a kingly blade: it gains +3 Strength per combat after enough elites are slain. Strong if you can fight elites anyway — and you should be fighting some.
- **Walk away** → nothing lost, nothing gained.

### Brain Leech

- **Let it latch on** → add one card of your choice from a pool to your deck. A free pick near the start of a run is enormous, even with the parasite attached.
- **Shoo it away** → no gain.

### Relic Trader

Swaps one of your relics for a new one. The trade is random, so evaluate honestly: trading a dead relic for anything is great, trading your engine for a mystery is not.

### The Fake Merchant

If you carry a throwable potion, you can fight it — beating the impostor pays out a pile of gold plus a relic. No throwable potion? Just leave.

## Card removal: the single best Act 1 purchase

One recurring Act 1 event lets you **remove a card from your deck for 75 gold**. In the current economy that is the cheapest, most reliable deck-thinning in a run, and it is close to an instant pick — a slimmer deck means your real cards show up more often. Our [deck-thinning guide](/basics/deck-thinning-guide) explains why this matters so much.

## Act 1 gamble events

### Luminous Choir

- **Pay gold** → a random relic. Good when your wallet is healthy; relics compound.
- **Remove two cards + receive a spore card** → the added card is a dud; skip this branch.

### The Round Tea Party

- **Drink** → full heal plus a royal venom relic that drains 4 HP after every combat. Powerful, but the upkeep tax gets scarier the longer your run goes.
- **Refuse** → no downside.

### The Lost Wisp

Grants an AOE ability card, but attaches a decay-flavored drawback. Take it if your deck needs board wipes and you can afford the downside; otherwise leave.

### Battleworn Dummy (don't miss this one)

A combat event: fight a **75 HP training dummy with 3 turns** to deal as much damage as possible. Winning pays gold and a relic; **failing costs nothing**. Fight it every single time — it is the only truly free lottery ticket in the spire, and it doubles as a DPS check for your deck.

## Priorities by act

- **Act 1:** removal, heals, safe upgrades. Take the egg, the seed and the statue; skip risky HP-gambling events.
- **Act 2:** your deck has a shape now, so gamble selectively — relic trade-ins and combat events pay off once your engine works.
- **Act 3:** grab every last fixed upgrade (the dummy, mask tombs, knowledge events) and shore up your deck's one missing piece.

Universal rule: below half health, never gamble on HP-loss events; a dead run collects no relics.
