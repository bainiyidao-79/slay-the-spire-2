<h3 class="sec">Mechanics</h3>
<p>thumb The Crystal Sphere minigame with the grid in the middle and the 2 size options for divining on the right</p>
<p>The event first presents the player with a choice between two options. Both of them lead to the same minigame in which the player uncovers a crystal sphere for rewards. In the center of the screen is an 11x11 grid with rounded corners. The tiles start of unrevealed and have to be revealed in order to uncover the hidden rewards. Most of the rewards are larger than a single 1x1 tile and have to be uncovered completely before gaining them.</p>
<p>The tiles are uncovered via the process of Divination. Depending on the option chosen at the beginning, the player can divine 3 or 6 times in total. The 2 options for divining are a Small Divination which uncovers only a single tile, and a Big Divination which reveals a 3x3 area. Both options count as 1 "use" for the total number of divinations. At the end of the minigame, the player is presented with a list of all the rewards they managed to find - these can be picked in any order or skipped entirely. The only exception to this is if uncovering the hidden curse Doubt, which is added instantly to the deck.</p>
<p>Note that even though the box for the relic is viewed from an angled perspective, it still has the size of a 4x4 square, meaning it is not possible to uncover it completely with only 3 divinations.</p>
