<h2 class="sec">Options &amp; Outcomes</h2>
<div class="opt"><b>Overcome</b><p class="sub">[Specific card] is removed from your deck.</p></div>
<div class="opt"><b>Hold On</b><p class="sub">Lose 3 HP. The card in the above option is randomized.</p></div>
<h3 class="sec">Mechanics</h3>
<p>Choosing Hold On replaces the listed card with another chosen at random, and the event continues to ask you to choose between these options until you choose Overcome.</p>
<p>Each time Hold On is selected, the HP cost increases by 1.</p>
<p>The card is chosen randomly from all your cards without Eternal.</p>
<p>The first card chosen will never be Basic rarity (unless all of your cards are Basic). Basic cards can be chosen during rerolls.</p>
<p>The same card will never be chosen twice (unless it has already offered to remove all other cards in your deck at least once).</p>
