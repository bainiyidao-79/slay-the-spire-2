<h2 class="sec">Options &amp; Outcomes</h2>
<div class="opt"><b>Insert Common Potion</b><p class="sub">Lose a specified Common potion. Obtain an Upgraded Common [Attack/Skill] card reward.</p></div>
<div class="opt"><b>Insert Uncommon Potion</b><p class="sub">Lose a specified Uncommon potion. Obtain an Upgraded Uncommon [Attack/Skill/Power] card reward.</p></div>
<div class="opt"><b>Insert Rare Potion</b><p class="sub">Lose a specified Rare potion. Obtain an Upgraded Rare [Attack/Skill/Power] card reward.</p></div>
<div class="opt"><b>Insert Event Potion</b><p class="sub">Lose a specified Event potion. Obtain an Upgraded Rare [Attack/Skill/Power] card reward. The only Event potions are Foul Potion and Glowwater Potion.</p></div>
<div class="opt"><b>Insert Token Potion</b><p class="sub">Lose a specified Token potion. Obtain an Upgraded Common [Attack/Skill] card reward. The only Token potion is Potion-Shaped Rock. The first 3 potions in your potion slots are chosen as options to insert, prioritizing the leftmost potions. This can include multiple potions of the same rarity. The rarity of the card reward depends on the rarity of the potion inserted. Each option randomly specifies whether it gives an Attack, Skill, or Power card reward.</p></div>
