<h2 class="sec">Options &amp; Outcomes</h2>
<p>This event lets you create a custom Mad Science card by choosing a card type and a rider effect.</p>
<h3 class="sec">Step 1: Choose Card Type</h3>
<p>Weapon Create an Attack. (Deal 12 damage.)</p>
<p>Protector Create a Skill. (Gain 8 Block.)</p>
<p>Gadget Create a Power.</p>
<p>Two of the three types are offered at random.</p>
<h3 class="sec">Step 2: Choose Rider Effect</h3>
<p>Two rider effects are offered at random from the type's pool:</p>
<p>Sapping — Apply 2 Weak. Apply 2 Vulnerable.</p>
<p>Violence — Hits 2 additional times.</p>
<p>Choking — Whenever you play a card this turn, the enemy loses 6 HP.</p>
<p>Energized — Gain 2 energy.</p>
<p>Wisdom — Draw 3 cards.</p>
<p>Chaos — Add a random card into your Hand. It's free to play this turn.</p>
<p>Expertise — Gain 2 Strength. Gain 2 Dexterity.</p>
<p>Curious — Powers cost 1 less.</p>
<p>Improvement — At the end of combat, Upgrade a random card.</p>
