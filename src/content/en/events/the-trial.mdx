<h2 class="sec">Options &amp; Outcomes</h2>
<h3 class="sec">Accept</h3>
<div class="opt"><b>Accept</b><p class="sub">Serve as today's Decider.</p></div>
<p>One of three trials is randomly selected. Every trial presents two verdicts:</p>
<div class="opt"><b>DECIDE: Guilty</b><p class="sub">Add Regret (curse) to your Deck. Obtain 2 random Relics.</p></div>
<div class="opt"><b>DECIDE: Innocent</b><p class="sub">Add Shame (curse) to your Deck. Upgrade 2 cards.</p></div>
<h3 class="sec">Noble Trial</h3>
<div class="opt"><b>DECIDE: Guilty</b><p class="sub">Heal 10 HP.</p></div>
<div class="opt"><b>DECIDE: Innocent</b><p class="sub">Add Regret (curse) to your Deck. Gain 300 Gold.</p></div>
<h3 class="sec">Nondescript Trial</h3>
<div class="opt"><b>DECIDE: Guilty</b><p class="sub">Add Doubt (curse) to your Deck. Gain 2 card rewards.</p></div>
<div class="opt"><b>DECIDE: Innocent</b><p class="sub">Add Doubt (curse) to your Deck. Transform 2 cards.</p></div>
<h3 class="sec">Reject</h3>
<div class="opt"><b>Reject</b><p class="sub">You are not allowed to Reject.</p></div>
<p>The player is asked again if they are willing to participate with Reject being replaced with Double Down.</p>
<div class="opt"><b>Double Down</b><p class="sub">Face lethal repercussions.</p></div>
<p>The player is instantly killed. Not selectable in multiplayer.</p>
