<h2 class="sec">Options &amp; Outcomes</h2>
<div class="opt"><b>Wongo's Bargain Bin</b><p class="sub">Pay 100 Gold. Obtain 1 random Common Relic.</p></div>
<p>Player also receives 32 Wongo Points.</p>
<div class="opt"><b>Wongo's Featured Item</b><p class="sub">Pay 200 Gold. Obtain [Rare Relic].</p></div>
<p>This relic is randomly selected from the rare relics in the standard relic pool, and will be displayed by name in the event option.</p>
<p>The relic can't be blacklisted from the Merchant.</p>
<p>Player also receives 16 Wongo Points.</p>
<div class="opt"><b>Wongo's Mystery Box</b><p class="sub">Pay 300 Gold. Obtain 3 random Relics after 5 combats.</p></div>
<p>Player obtains Wongo's Mystery Ticket.</p>
<p>Player also receives 8 Wongo Points.</p>
<div class="opt"><b>Leave</b><p class="sub">Downgrade a random card.</p></div>
<h3 class="sec">Wongo Points</h3>
<p>After any purchase, the player will receive Wongo Points based on which purchase they selected. Wongo Points persist between all of the player's runs on their profile (across all characters), and the event text will display how many total Wongo Points the player has after making a purchase, as well as how many more points are needed to reach 2000 total points.</p>
<p>Once the player reaches 2000 total Wongo Points, they will receive the Wongo Customer Appreciation Badge, which does nothing.</p>
