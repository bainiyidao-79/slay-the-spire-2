<h2 class="sec">Options &amp; Outcomes</h2>
<div class="opt"><b>Wongo's Bargain Bin</b><p class="sub">Pay 100 Gold. Obtain 1 random Common Relic. Player also receives 32 Wongo Points.</p></div>
<div class="opt"><b>Wongo's Featured Item</b><p class="sub">Pay 200 Gold. Obtain [Rare Relic]. This relic is randomly selected from the rare relics in the standard relic pool, and will be displayed by name in the event option. The relic can't be blacklisted from the Merchant. Player also receives 16 Wongo Points.</p></div>
<div class="opt"><b>Wongo's Mystery Box</b><p class="sub">Pay 300 Gold. Obtain 3 random Relics after 5 combats. Player obtains Wongo's Mystery Ticket. Player also receives 8 Wongo Points.</p></div>
