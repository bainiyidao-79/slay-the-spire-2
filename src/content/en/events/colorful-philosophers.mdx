</div></div></div>


<h2 class="sec">Options &amp; Outcomes</h2>
<p>Up to 3 options are shown, chosen from other characters' card pools (excluding your current character). Each option offers 3 card rewards (1 Common, 1 Uncommon, 1 Rare) from that character's pool:</p>
<p>Red Obtain 3 Ironclad cards.</p>
<p>Green Obtain 3 Silent cards.</p>
<p>Blue Obtain 3 Defect cards.</p>
<p>Pink Obtain 3 Necrobinder cards.</p>
<p>Orange Obtain 3 Regent cards.</p>
<h3 class="sec">Interactions</h3>
<p>If the player has Prismatic Gem and/or Dingy Rug: If the player has Prismatic Gem, then regardless of the option chosen, the resulting card rewards will include cards from all the characters, including the current character. If the player has Dingy Rug, the resulting card rewards will include Colorless cards in addition to cards of the chosen color. If the player has Prismatic Gem and Dingy Rug, then whichever Relic was picked up first will impose its respective card rewards modification, and the other will not. E.g. if Prismatic Gem was picked up first, then the card rewards will include cards from all the characters, including the current character, but not include Colorless cards.</p>
<p>Driftwood allows the card rewards to be rerolled.</p>
<p>Pael's Wing allows the card rewards to be sacrificed for an eventual Relic.</p>
<p>Wing Charm applies Swift 1 to one of the cards in each reward.</p>
<p>Molten Egg, Toxic Egg, and Frozen Egg upgrade their respective card types as intended.</p>
