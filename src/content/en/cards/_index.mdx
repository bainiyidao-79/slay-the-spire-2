## How Tiers Are Assigned

S = premium, take it almost always · A = strong, usually correct · B = solid role-player · C = situational, often a trap · D = avoid without a specific plan. Rankings come from the September 2026 community card table cross-checked with the full card-by-card review — see the [Tier List](/cards/tier-list) for the methodology and the [Patch Notes](/news/patch-notes) for what moved this week.
