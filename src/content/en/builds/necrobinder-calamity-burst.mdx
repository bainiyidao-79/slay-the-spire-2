**Doom** is the Necrobinder's most sinister mechanic — a stored kill counter. Every stack sits on the enemy, and the game checks it at the end of their turn: *if an enemy's Doom is equal to or higher than its remaining HP, it dies.* Block, armor, shields — irrelevant. This build stacks Doom by the hundreds and files the paperwork later.

## The stacking core

- **No Escape** (Uncommon, 1) — apply 10 (15) Doom, **plus 5 more for every 10 Doom already on the target**. The snowball: deep stacks accelerate themselves.
- **Negative Pulse** (Common, 1) — gain 5 (6) Block, apply 7 (11) Doom to ALL enemies. Defense and board-wide stacking in one line; take two.
- **Deathbringer** (Uncommon, 2) — 21 (26) Doom plus 1 Weak to ALL enemies. The board-clearer among stackers.
- **End of Days** (Rare, 3) — apply 29 (37) Doom to ALL enemies **and kill anything whose Doom meets its HP immediately**. The mass-execution button.

## The executor

- **Time's Up** (Rare, 2) — **deal damage equal to the enemy's Doom**, with a Retain upgrade. The finisher that converts 200 stored stacks into 200 damage right now, instead of waiting for the enemy's upkeep. Bosses die to this card on schedule.

## The defense package

Stacking turns are also walls in this deck:

- **Shroud** (Power, 1) — whenever you apply Doom, gain 3 (4) Block. Every stacker is now a fortifier.
- **Death's Door** (Uncommon, 1) — gain 6 (7) Block, **two extra times if you applied Doom this turn** — which is every turn. Forty-plus Block from one energy.
- **Bone Flute** (relic-grade support) — Osty's attacks grant 2 Block each; the summon stays useful while you count down.

## Turn template

1. **Wall and spread:** Negative Pulse — Block plus Doom across the pack.
2. **Focus:** No Escape on the priority target; the per-10 bonus punishes every earlier stack.
3. **Execute:** End of Days clears everything the room's Doom already covers; Time's Up deletes the boss the moment stacks pass its health line.

## Deck discipline

Aim for 20–25 cards with the four stackers, Time's Up, Shroud, and two Death's Doors as the spine. What you decline matters: non-Doom damage decks dilute the kill check, and healing-free aggression is wasted when the execution does not care how much HP the enemy has left. See [deck thinning](/basics/deck-thinning-guide) — this deck wants its stackers, its walls, and nothing else.
