**Hellraiser** is the Ironclad's most dangerous Power: *"Whenever you draw a card containing 'Strike', it is played against a random enemy."* Every Strike you draw throws itself at the enemy for free — energy, hand slots and effort all optional. Build the deck around Strike density and draw, and combat becomes a machine that fires while you watch.

## The engine

- **Hellraiser** (Power, 2, Rare) — the automation switch. One copy is all you need; a second is fine but never required.
- **Perfected Strike** (2) — 6 damage, **plus 2 (3) for every card in your deck containing "Strike"**, itself included. This is the build's scaling payoff: every Strike you add raises every Perfected Strike you draw.
- **Pommel Strike ×2–3** (1) — 9 (10) damage, draw 1 (2). Feeds Hellraiser while drawing you deeper.
- **Twin Strike** (1) — 5 (7) twice. Cheap Strike count that Perfected Strike and Hellraiser both love.

## Draw that keeps the gun spinning

- **Pillage** (1) — 6 (9) damage, then draw until a non-Attack. The best single card for finding Strikes.
- **Battle Trance** (0) — draw 3 (4). A mini-turn on demand.
- **Shrug It Off** (1) — 8 (11) Block, draw 1. The only block the deck really wants.
- **Burning Pact** (1) — Exhaust 1 card, draw 2 (3). Compresses the deck toward Strikes only.

## Deckbuilding rules

1. **Say yes to Strikes.** This is the one Ironclad build that hoards them — Strike count is literally Perfected Strike's damage stat.
2. **Delete Defends, not Strikes**, at shops and events. The pile should trend toward attacks.
3. Keep it to **20 cards or fewer**; Hellraiser needs you to redraw your Strikes often.
4. One Hellraiser, two to four Perfected Strikes, the Pommel Strike line, then fill with draw.

## Turn template

Cast Hellraiser early (it is a 2-cost Power — find it with draw, not patience), then spend the turn playing draw: Pommel Strike, Pillage, Battle Trance. Every Strike those effects drag out of the pile fires itself at a random enemy, Perfected Strikes among them scale off the pile's density, and your actual energy goes to block. The dream turn is Pillage into four auto-firing Strikes into a dead hallway fight.

## Watch the failure modes

- **Random targeting.** Hellraiser hits *random* enemies, so wide packs blunt it — bring a Whirlwind or two for AoE weeks, and treat Hellraiser turns as single-target tech.
- **The build is thin on block.** Second Wind converts junk into a wall when a fight drags; don't let the machine pride talk you out of blocking.
- **Perfected Strike needs density time.** Early in a run it is mediocre; by Act 2 with 15+ Strikes it is the best card in the deck. Patience, then detonation.
