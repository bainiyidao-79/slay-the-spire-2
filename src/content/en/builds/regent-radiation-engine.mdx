**Radiate** is the Regent's loudest card: *"Deal 3 (4) damage to ALL enemies for each Star gained this turn."* Note the wording — not stars *held*, stars **gained this turn**. The whole build is a promise to yourself: bank nothing, generate everything, and on the burst turn stack so many Star gains in a single pass that the board simply leaves.

## The burst core

- **Radiate ×2** (Uncommon) — the terminal. Both copies matter: one burst turn per cycle is good, two is a schedule. Upgrade first.
- **Genesis** (Rare Power) — gain 2 (3) Stars at the start of each turn. Free interest that every Radiate counts.
- **Royal Gamble** (Uncommon) — gain **9 Stars**, Exhaust, with a Retain upgrade. The jackpot card: play it on a burst turn and Radiate's multiplier jumps nine in one card.
- **Glow** (Common) — gain 1 (2) Stars, draw 1, draw 1 next turn. Generation attached to velocity.
- **Solar Strike** (Common) — 9 (10) damage, gain a Star. Damage that funds the multiplier.
- **Gather Light** (Common) — 8 (11) Block and a Star. Defense that still counts toward the burst.

## The turn-1-of-the-fight setup

- **Black Hole** (Uncommon Power) — 3 (4) damage to ALL whenever you spend or gain Stars. Passive AOE that turns every future generation turn into chip damage while you set up.
- **Convergence** (Uncommon) — next turn gain energy and Stars, retain your hand. The pre-burst button: it loads tomorrow's Radiate turn with resources today.
- **Walls:** Particle Wall (9 (12) Block, returns to hand) is the standing shield between setup and payday.

## The burst turn, step by step

1. **Open with Convergence-prepped energy** — this turn is funded by yesterday.
2. **Stack generation:** Royal Gamble (+9), Glow (+1/2), Gather Light (+1), Solar Strike (+1), Genesis' turn tick (+2/3) — each gain is also Black Hole damage while it lands.
3. **Cast Radiate.** Ten Stars gained means 30 (40) base to ALL enemies per copy — before Vulnerable, before Strength, with a second Radiate behind it.
4. **Sweep leftovers** with Stardust (X-times random hits) or Astral Pulse (6 (8) to ALL twice).

## Deckbuilding and route notes

Keep 20–22 cards, majority star economy: two Radiate, generation commons, one Black Hole, walls, and a thin draw line. **Do not chase Forge or Sovereign Blade packages** — they are powerful elsewhere and dead weight here. Act 1 is about surviving with commons (Astral Pulse, Crescent Spear — which scales per Star-cost card in your deck) while the engine assembles; by the time Act 2 elites arrive, the first Radiate turn should be online.
