Uniquely among the five characters, the Ironclad wants his starting Strikes — enchantments, **Strike Dummy** and Vulnerability scaling turn them into a real engine.

## The Strikes Strategy (Why Ironclad Keeps His Deck)

Uniquely among the five characters, the Ironclad **wants his starting Strikes**: Strength and Vulnerability scale them, **Strike Dummy** multiplies them, and enchantments can make them free (the guide's example: a soup enchantment that makes every Strike cost zero, then draw your whole deck and play them all). Don't auto-remove Strikes the way you would on other characters.
