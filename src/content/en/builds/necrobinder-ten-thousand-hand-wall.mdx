The **ten-thousand HP hand** is the Necrobinder's answer to being fragile: instead of fixing her 66 HP, grow **Osty** into a wall with a health bar the boss cannot chew through — then mirror that bar back at the enemy team. Osty tanks, Osty swings, and the lich scrolls her deck behind him.

## The scaling package

- **Bodyguard** (Basic, 1) — Summon 5 (7). Your starter's worth finally explained: every Summon stack is an Osty HP point, and this is the first.
- **Sic 'Em** (Uncommon, 1) — Osty deals 5 (6) damage, and **whenever Osty hits that enemy this turn, Summon 3 (4)**. Attack turns now feed the health pool.
- **Devour Life** (Rare Power) — every Soul played Summons 1 (2). Pairs with the [soul engine](/builds/necrobinder-soul-drain); production turns become scaling turns.
- **Necro Mastery** (Rare Power, 2) — Summon 5 (8), and **whenever Osty loses HP, ALL enemies lose that much too**. The build's thesis statement: hitting the hand is now a team-wide tax.

## The cash-out attacks

- **Squeeze** (Rare, 3) — Osty deals 25 (30) damage, plus 5 (6) for each of your other Osty attacks this turn. The "one punch" finisher: with the hand scaled and a few cheap swings first, this deletes bosses.
- **Snap** (Common, 1) — Osty deals 7 (10) damage and adds Retain to a card in hand. Cheap, scaling, and hand-quality in one.
- **Reap** (Common, 3) — Retain; deal 27 (33) damage. The biggest common attack in the game — hold it, fire it, win the trade war.

## The defensive shell

- **Bone Flute** — 2 Block whenever Osty attacks. His aggression is your shield.
- **Sentry Mode** (Rare Power) — adds a recurring utility card to your hand each turn; upgrade priority for consistency.
- **Death's Door / Shroud** from the [Doom package](/builds/necrobinder-calamity-burst) slot in cleanly if rewards offer them — this deck has room for one engine only, but never for zero walls.

## Turn template

1. **Scale early:** Bodyguard, Sic 'Em, Devour Life turns — every Summon stack is permanent until Osty falls, and he revives every turn with Bound Phylactery anyway.
2. **Park him in front:** enemies that attack the frontmost target bleed into Osty's pool; Necro Mastery reflects it.
3. **Cash:** once the pool is monstrous, Squeeze (fed by Snap swings) lands for the whole ledger.

The build's rule is patience: Osty's HP is cumulative equity, and every defensive turn is a deposit. Enemies swing the hand, the hand grows, and eventually the lich does not even need to block — the tanky left hand is the deck.
