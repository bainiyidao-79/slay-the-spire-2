The Vulnerable stack plan is the smoothest high-ceiling Ironclad route in Early Access: apply Vulnerable fast, convert it to Strength with **Dominate**, multiply with **Cruelty**, and finish with one enormous hit. This variant leans on the sturdiest openers — Bash, Tremble and Squash — so the deck survives long enough to cash in.

## The setup core

- **Bash** (2) — 8 (10) damage and apply 2 (3) Vulnerable. Your starting card and still the most reliable opener; upgrade it early.
- **Tremble** (1) — apply 3 (4) Vulnerable, Exhaust. Pure setup, gone before it clogs the deck.
- **Squash** (1, event reward) — 10 (12) damage and 2 (3) Vulnerable. If the event offers it, it is a one-card opening hand.
- **Dominate** (1, Rare) — apply Vulnerable, then **gain 1 Strength per Vulnerable on the enemy**. The conversion step; take it on sight and upgrade it first.

## The multipliers

- **Cruelty** (Power, 1) — Vulnerable enemies take an extra 25% (50%) damage. The reason one converted hit kills.
- **Molten Fist** (1) — 10 (14) damage, **double the enemy's Vulnerable**, Exhaust. Late fight, doubled stacks, one more Dominate: Strength goes vertical.
- **Colossus** (1) — gain 4 (7) Block, and **you take 50% less damage from Vulnerable enemies this turn**. Defense that keys off your own setup — the build's safety net.

## Draw and energy

- **Pillage** (1) — 6 (9) damage, then draw until you hit a non-Attack. Filters the deck toward the payload.
- **Headbutt** (1) — 9 (12) damage and puts a card from your discard pile on top of your draw pile. Keeps Dominate on schedule.
- **Bloodletting** (0) — lose 3 HP, gain 2 (3) energy. The loop's fuel.

## Turn template

1. **Stack:** Bash / Tremble / Squash load Vulnerable.
2. **Convert:** Dominate turns stacks into Strength.
3. **Amplify:** Cruelty online; Molten Fist doubles the stack count if it is still alive.
4. **Cash:** one big attack — or Whirlwind for groups — rides Strength, Cruelty and Vulnerable's 50% weakness at the same time.

Boss fights are the same script with one extra beat: hold Molten Fist until after the boss's big attack, then double and convert on the same turn.

## Rules for drafting it

- Take **every** Dominate you are offered (one is enough, two is a luxury), upgrade it first at campfires.
- **Paper Phrog** (Vulnerable enemies take 75% more instead of 50%) and **Vajra** (start combat with 1 Strength) are the relics this deck dreams about.
- Skip strikes you do not need; the deck wants a lean, mean conversion loop, not a bigger pile.
