Not every Regent run is an infinite loop. The **starlight engine** is the honest midrange version: pair every star spender with a star producer, keep the ledger balanced, and let the class's high-value attack cards carry you while the economy scales. It is the build that teaches you how the Regent actually works — including his brutal early game.

## The ledger rule

Star cards come in two families — **spenders** (attacks that cost Stars and hit hard) and **producers** (skills that gain Stars). The deck works when the totals roughly balance: **for every spender, one producer**. A deck of spenders starves; a deck of producers never converts economy into damage. Every reward screen, run the ledger before picking.

## The cards that anchor it

**Producers:**

- **Glow** — gain 1 (2) Stars, draw 1, draw 1 next turn. The best producer per cost.
- **Gather Light** — 8 (11) Block plus a Star. Defense on the ledger.
- **Solar Strike** — 9 (10) damage and a Star. The producer that fights.
- **Genesis** (Rare Power) — 2 (3) Stars at the start of every turn. The passive that turns the economy on.
- **Royal Gamble** — gain 9 Stars, Exhaust. The flash flood.

**Spenders and value:**

- **Stardust** — deal 5 (7) to a random enemy X times. Converts stored Stars into flexible damage.
- **Seven Stars** — 7 damage to ALL enemies, seven times. The AOE ceiling.
- **Crescent Spear** — 8 damage, plus 2 (3) for every Star-cost card in your deck. A common that rewards the archetype itself.
- **Black Hole** (Power) — every Star spent or gained deals 3 (4) to ALL enemies. The engine's passive tax collector.

## Route planning around the weak Act 1

The Regent's opening act is the roughest in Early Access, so the map matters more than usual:

1. **Campfires first, elites last.** Early Regent needs upgrades and removals more than relics; plan the act around the fire icons.
2. **Fewer question marks in Act 1, more in Act 2.** Early events gamble a fragile life total; Act 2 events pay a formed deck.
3. **Shop timing:** the back half of an act pays better — prices are the same, your deck is better at using them. Buy potions and key spenders if an early shop is forced.
4. **Take strong commons over engine pieces in the first ten cards.** Astral Pulse and Crescent Spear win fights now; the engine finishes later.

## The scaling curve

Act 1 survives on high-value commons and potions. Act 2 completes the producer/spender ledger and adds Genesis or Black Hole. Act 3 is where the build's identity arrives: every turn generates, every spend echoes through Black Hole, and Seven Stars or Stardust empties rooms. The deck never stops being a spreadsheet — it just becomes a spreadsheet with a crown.
