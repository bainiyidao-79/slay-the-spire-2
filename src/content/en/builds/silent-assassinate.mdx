The **assassinate** build is the Silent as a contract killer: open the fight with damage the enemy never sees coming, apply Weak so their answers whiff, then cash one enormous Finisher window. Unlike the pure shiv decks, this is a tempo deck — fewer knives, each one aimed.

## The opener package (innate damage)

- **Backstab** (Uncommon) — Innate: it starts in your opening hand. 11 (15) damage, Exhaust. A free, guaranteed hit before the fight begins.
- **Neutralize** (Basic) — 3 (4) damage and 1 (2) Weak. Your starter, and still the cheapest Weak application in the game.
- **Sucker Punch** (Common) — 8 (10) damage, apply 1 (2) Weak. Extends the Weak coverage into later turns.

## The multiplier package

- **Finisher** (Uncommon) — deal 6 (8) damage for each Attack already played this turn. The build's namesake payoff: five attacks first means Finisher lands for 30-plus.
- **Tracking** (Power, Rare) — **Weak enemies take 50% more damage from Attacks.** The reason every list below applies Weak.
- **Noxious Fumes** (Power) — 2 (3) Poison to ALL enemies at the start of your turn. Passive chip that finishes whatever the burst misses.
- **Envenom** (Power, Rare) — unblocked Attack hits apply 1 (2) Poison. Turns the attack volume into a second damage layer.

## The knife base

You still want shiv generators — **Blade Dance**, **Cloak and Dagger**, **Up My Sleeve** — not as the win condition but as Finisher ammunition: each Shiv played counts toward Finisher's attack tally. **Accuracy** remains welcome; a +6 Shiv is a fine cheap attack.

## The Weak engine

If Tracking is in the deck, Weak coverage is mandatory: Neutralize and Sucker Punch are the baseline, **Leg Sweep** (Weak + 11 (14) Block) doubles as the build's best defensive card, and **Malaise** (Rare) strips an elite's Strength while applying big Weak when X-cost energy lines up.

## The kill window

1. **Turn one:** Backstab fires automatically; Neutralize applies Weak; shiv generation sets the tally.
2. **Turn two:** more attacks into the Weakened target, Tracking multiplying everything; Noxious Fumes and Envenom stack the exit wound.
3. **The window:** Finisher — attack count times 6 (8), doubled by the target's Weak through Tracking, with poison already ticking underneath. Elites die inside this window; bosses lose a quarter of their bar per cycle.

The deck's discipline is patience with rewards: shiv generators, Weak coverage and the three Powers are the shopping list. A flashy rare attack that doesn't attack multiple times, apply Weak, or generate shivs is someone else's build.
