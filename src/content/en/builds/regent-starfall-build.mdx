Stockpile Stars and unload them in one turn — the Starfall nuke is the Regent's signature blowout.

## Starfall — the Star Nuke

**Play pattern:** stockpile Stars and Vigor across a turn or two, then cash everything in at once — most reliably through **Radiate**, which deals damage to every enemy for each Star you gained this turn (3 damage per Star by default). With enough setup the video shows Radiate going from 3 to 52 damage in a single turn after banking 25 Stars. If you've played Magic: The Gathering, it plays like a storm deck: build up, then kill everything in one go. Starfall is the most reliable archetype community play has found — the backup payoff is **Stardust**, a spend-X damage card (cited at 5 damage per hit), weaker in multi-enemy fights.

**Core cards:**

| Card | Cost | Effect | Why it earns a slot |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Radiate | — | Damage to all enemies for every Star gained this turn (base 3 per Star) | The best Starfall payoff — an AoE nuke that scales without limit |
| Royal Gamble | 5 | Gains 9 Stars | Almost mandatory: 9 Stars makes Radiate a 27-damage AoE; take it even as a dead draw |
| Hidden Cache | — | The highest base Star generation in the kit | A setup turn of +3 Stars before the Radiate turn; also pairs with the 5–6 cost Star cards |
| Genesis | — | Sustained Star engine; the upgrade gives 3 Stars per turn | Passive Star every turn means passive Radiate damage every turn |
| Knockout Blow | 3 | 30 damage; gain 5 Stars if you kill something | Feeds Radiate (5 Stars = 15 AoE damage); off your starting 3 Stars it opens fights at 9 AoE damage |
| Vigor | — | Gain 6 Vigor (8 upgraded); Vigor adds that much to your next attack, per hit | Pushes Radiate from 3 to 11 damage per Star — 9 Stars becomes 99 damage |
| Terraforming | — | Another Vigor source for stacking | Vigor persists across turns as long as you don't attack, so you can bank it |

**Relics:** the material's relic advice for the Regent is thin — cards simply matter more. Crown of Stars (3 Stars at combat start, 6 upgraded) is the engine every Starfall plan uses, and the explicit advice is to push card rewards and shops first, relic slots second.

**How to pilot it:** the dream line is Decisions, Decisions into Royal Gamble — roughly 27 Stars in one turn, which kills almost anything with a small damage boost. Stack Vigor patiently (Patter, Terraforming), don't attack while banking it, and block until the big turn. If Radiate never shows up, **Lunar Blast** is a worse version that only makes sense with Decisions, Decisions (doubled into itself, replaying Monologue/Terraforming for Strength — the video's best case reached ~600 damage) or as an infinite-deck payoff.

**Strengths and weaknesses:** scales to absurd numbers and crushes bosses; but it leans on specific payoffs, and without Radiate/Royal Gamble you need a lot of sustained generation to get there.
