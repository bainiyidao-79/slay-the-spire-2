**Sly** is the Silent's quietest mechanic and the loudest once it starts: cards with Sly go free when triggered, and the Power **Master Planner** (*"When you play a Skill, it gains Sly"*) turns every Skill you play this combat into a permanently free card. The Sly loop deck weaponizes that: skills that cast for free, draw that never runs out, and a Burst-closed cycle that empties your deck into the enemy's face in a single turn.

## The engine

- **Master Planner** (Power) — when you play a Skill, it gains Sly. One copy is the whole build's ignition: Acrobatics cast once is free forever after.
- **Acrobatics ×2–3** (1) — draw 3 (4), discard 1. Once Sly, a draw-4 engine that costs nothing.
- **Prepared ×2** (0) — draw 1 (2), discard 1 (2). Free fuel with discard synergy attached.
- **Burst** (Rare) — your next 1 (2) Skills this turn are played an extra time. The loop lock: a Bursted Acrobatics draws eight and leaves two free copies in hand.
- **Tactician** (Uncommon) — energy when discarded. Funds the first turn before Master Planner comes online.

## The payload while the loop spins

- **Dagger Throw** (Common) — 9 (12) damage, draw 1, discard 1. An attack that keeps cycling.
- **Up My Sleeve** — add 3 (4) Shivs, cost drops by 1 each play. Eventually free damage every loop pass.
- **Cloak and Dagger** — 6 Block, add 1 (2) Shivs. Turns the loop into defense automatically.
- **Afterimage** (Power, Rare) — 1 Block per card played. In a full loop this is a fortress you never bought.

## Deck plan (18–22 cards)

The deck must be pure: Master Planner, the Acrobatics/Prepared cycle package, Burst, payload skills, and almost nothing else. Basic Strikes and high-cost attacks are dead weight — a non-Skill card in hand is a loop-breaker, not a resource. Delete aggressively; every removal raises the loop's stability. (See [deck thinning](/basics/deck-thinning-guide).)

## How the turn plays

1. **Ignite:** cast Master Planner. From now on, every Skill you play gains Sly — free for the rest of the combat.
2. **Cycle:** Acrobatics draws, Prepared filters, discards pay Tactician. The hand refills faster than it empties.
3. **Lock:** Burst duplicates the loop cards — two Acrobatics for the price of one is where the chain stops ending.
4. **Cash:** free Dagger Throws and Shivs hammer the board while Afterimage quietly walls you to full block.

The failure mode is simply not finding Master Planner: the deck without its Power is a mediocre draw deck. Tutors don't exist, so take two cycle density cards early, shop deliberately, and remember the loop only needs to start once — Sly never expires.
