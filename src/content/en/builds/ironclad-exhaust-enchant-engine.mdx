Exhaust decks sound like a contradiction — win by destroying your own cards? — but the Ironclad's burn package is one of Early Access's most satisfying engines: **Rupture** pays you Strength for every HP you burn, **Feel No Pain** pays block for every card exhausted, and **Fiend Fire** turns the whole hand into one final detonation. Slay the Spire 2's new **Enchantment** system adds a fresh layer on top.

<div class="art-ph lead"><img src="/images/sts2-event-blessing.webp" loading="eager" alt="A mystical event scene in Slay the Spire 2"><figcaption>Every card you burn is Strength in the bank and Block in the pocket.</figcaption></div>

## The payoffs

- **Rupture** (Power, 1) — whenever you lose HP on your turn, gain 1 (2) Strength. The engine's profit center; every self-damage card now scales you.
- **Feel No Pain** (Power, 1) — whenever a card is Exhausted, gain 3 (4) Block. The exhaust pile becomes a shield generator.
- **Fiend Fire** (2, Rare) — **Exhaust your entire hand; deal 7 (10) damage per card Exhausted.** The finisher: a full hand at the right moment is 50+ damage from one card.
- **Corruption** (Power, 3, Ancient rarity) — all Skills cost 0, but every Skill played Exhausts. Insane acceleration if your skills are the payload.

## The fuel

- **Bloodletting** (0) — lose 3 HP, gain 2 (3) energy. Draws a card's worth of Rupture Strength for free.
- **Hemokinesis** (1) — lose 2 HP, deal 15 (20). A burn card that is already a real attack.
- **Offering** (0, Rare) — lose 6 HP, gain 2 energy, draw 3 (5), Exhaust. Burst-draw that stacks Strength through Rupture on the way.
- **Burning Pact** (1) — Exhaust 1 card, draw 2 (3). Compression that feeds Feel No Pain.
- **Second Wind** (1) — Exhaust all non-Attacks in hand, gain 5 (7) Block each. Defense, thinning, and Feel No Pain triggers in one line.
- **Forgotten Ritual** (1) — gain 3 (4) energy, Exhaust. The burst-turn starter.

## The Enchantment layer

Slay the Spire 2's **Enchantment system** adds modifiers to specific cards in your deck — event and enemy rewards like the Symbiote's **Corrupted** enchant (an attack deals 50% more damage but costs 2 HP) slot perfectly here: the extra HP loss is free Strength through Rupture. When an enchant choice appears, prefer the option on a card you exhaust or replay often; the enchant survives even though the card may not.

## Running it

1. **Rupture first.** Every other card in the deck is fuel; the Power that converts fuel to Strength is the build.
2. **Burn on schedule, not all at once.** Spread Bloodletting/Hemokinesis across turns so Feel No Pain's block keeps arriving.
3. **Count your hand before Fiend Fire.** Ten damage per card means a six-card hand is a boss bar; don't fire it on a three-card hand.
4. **Respect the exhaust pile.** What is gone is gone — block effects and Battle Trance-style draw spikes should be spent before you burn them.

The deck's rhythm — burn, scale, wall up, detonate — carries it through elite fights that would bully a plain attack deck, and Corruption turns the late game into a slot machine that always pays.
