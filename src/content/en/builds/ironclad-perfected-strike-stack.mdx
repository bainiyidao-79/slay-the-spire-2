Most Ironclad builds hunt for a rare Power to switch the lights on. The **Perfected Strike stack** doesn't wait: it wins by inflating a common. Every card in your deck containing "Strike" raises **Perfected Strike's** damage — collect enough of them, upgrade them with **Armaments**, and a 2-cost common becomes the hardest single hit in the run.

## The payoff card

**Perfected Strike** (2, Common) — deal 6 damage, **plus 2 (3) for every card containing "Strike" in your deck**, counting copies of itself and every basic Strike you never removed. This is the rare deck that *wants* its starting Strikes: each one is +2 damage on every copy, forever.

The stacking rules of thumb:

- Five-plus copies of Perfected Strike are realistic by Act 2 — card rewards and events offer it often because it is a common.
- Upgraded copies add +3 per Strike instead of +2, so campfire upgrades compound quadratically with deck density.
- Ten Strikes in the pile turns an upgraded Perfected Strike into 36 damage — before Strength, before Vulnerable, every single copy.

## The support line

- **Armaments** (1) — gain 5 Block, **upgrade a card in your hand** (all cards when upgraded). In-place upgrades without campfires; upgraded Strikes also hit harder through their own base damage.
- **Pommel Strike** (1) — 9 (10) damage, draw 1 (2). A Strike that filters toward your other Perfected Strikes.
- **Twin Strike** (1) — 5 (7) twice. Density plus chip damage.
- **Anger** (0) — 6 (8) damage, adds a copy into your discard pile. A Strike that grows its own count.
- **Headbutt** (1) — 9 (12) damage, stacks your discard on top of the draw pile. Digs a Perfected Strike back out.
- **Bloodletting** (0) — lose 3 HP, gain 2 (3) energy. Perfected Strike costs 2; this is how you cast two in a turn.

## What this deck refuses

- **Exhaust effects on Strikes** — the pile is the stat line; permanently deleting Strikes is self-sabotage.
- **Non-Strike attacks**, mostly — a rare Whirlwind for AoE weeks is fine, but every non-Strike slot is a missed +2.
- **Big deck bloat without Strikes** — draw 5 and you want at least two Strikes per draw; that means the pile stays Strikes-plus-draw, 20 cards max.

## Piloting

Turn one is setup: Bloodletting, Armaments if the hand has targets, chip damage. The payoff turn is any hand with two Perfected Strikes — 12 base, +2 per Strike each, upgraded math on top; with Strength from a Inflame-style card and a Vulnerable opener like Bash, single hits cross 60 against bosses. It is the least flashy of the Ironclad's engines and one of the most reliable, because it never needs a rare to come online.
