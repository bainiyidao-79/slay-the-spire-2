This is the Defect build the class was designed around: **Frost** orbs for armor, **Lightning** orbs for burst, and **Focus** — the stat that multiplies every orb's numbers — as the scaling axis. Frost keeps you alive early, Focus compounds the engine, and by the late game a full rack of slots detonates through **Tesla Coil** and **Thunder** before the enemy's second turn.

## The Focus engine

- **Focused Strike** (Uncommon, 1) — 9 (11) damage, gain 1 (2) Focus **this turn**. Attack and temp-Focus in one line.
- **Hotfix** (1) — gain 2 Focus this turn, Exhaust. The instant-dial burst enabler.
- **Defragment** (Rare Power, 1) — gain 1 (2) Focus permanently for the combat. The scaling anchor.
- **Biased Cognition** (Ancient Power, 1) — gain **5 (6) Focus** at the cost of 1 per turn afterward. The biggest single Focus swing in the game; four or five turns of god-mode orbs wins most fights before the decay matters.
- **Synchronize** — Focus this turn for each unique orb type you have. The hybrid-rack payoff.

## Orb mechanics, precisely

Per the game's own keyword rules: a **Frost** orb's passive grants 2 (+ Focus) Block, and evoking it grants 5 (+ Focus) Block. **Lightning** passives fire damage at a random enemy when triggered. Every point of Focus raises all of these numbers at once — which is why Focus is the build's whole economy.

## Filling the rack

- **Ball Lightning** (Common, 1) — 7 (10) damage and Channel 1 Lightning. The best common in the class.
- **Storm** (Uncommon Power, 1) — whenever you play a Power, Channel 1 (2) Lightning. Powers become passive generation.
- **Tempest** (Uncommon, X) — Channel X (X+1) Lightning. A full rack in one cast on an energy-rich turn.
- **Capacitor** (Uncommon Power, 1) — gain 2 (3) orb slots. More slots is more simultaneous passive income.
- **Rainbow** (Rare, 2) — Channel Lightning, Frost and Dark. The flex slot filler.

## The cash-out

- **Tesla Coil** (Uncommon, 0) — deal 3 (4) damage and **trigger ALL your Lightning orbs against the enemy** (twice when upgraded). The burst button: a rack of six Lightning orbs becomes a targeted salvo.
- **Thunder** (Uncommon Power, 1) — whenever you Evoke Lightning, deal 8 (11) damage to each enemy hit. Turns slot turnover into AOE.
- **Loop / Hyperbeam** — passive-doubling and Focus-spenders for the late fights.

## Building and piloting

Act 1: Ball Lightning, Charge Battery (7 (10) Block plus next-turn energy), and any Frost channel you see — survive while shopping for Focus. Act 2: Defragment or Biased Cognition comes online; every channel now gains value permanently. Act 3: slots full (5–6), Focus 5+, and every turn ends in Tesla Coil salvos with Thunder echoing them across packs. Keep 25–30 cards at most; orbs hate dead draws as much as any engine (see [deck thinning](/basics/deck-thinning-guide)).
