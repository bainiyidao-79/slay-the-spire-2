The **blade-poison hybrid** wins the argument between shiv players and poison players by refusing to choose: shivs land the hits, and every unblocked hit applies poison through **Envenom**. The finale is **Knife Trap** — *"Upgrade and play every Shiv in your Exhaust Pile on the enemy"* — which converts a whole game's worth of spent knives into one upgraded firing squad.

## The engine core

- **Knife Trap** (Uncommon) — (Upgraded:) **upgrade and play every Shiv in your Exhaust Pile** against one enemy. A late-game card: the longer the fight, the bigger the exhaust pile, the harder this hits — hundreds of damage from a single line.
- **Envenom** (Power, Rare) — whenever an Attack deals unblocked damage, apply 1 (2) Poison. Every shiv hit now stacks poison on the target — even the replayed ones.
- **Accuracy** (Power) — Shivs deal +4 (6). Feeds both halves: shiv hits get bigger, poison gets applied more reliably past Block.

## The trigger multipliers

- **Pen Nib** (relic) — every 10th Attack deals double damage. Knife Trap's replay counts toward it; time your tenth attack for the Trap.
- **Nunchaku** (Uncommon relic) — every 10 Attacks played grants 1 energy. High-volume shiv turns refuel the loop.
- **Joss Paper** (relic) — every 5 cards Exhausted, draw 1. Exhausted shivs become card advantage instead of a cost.
- **Serpent Form** (Power) — deal 4 (6) to a random enemy whenever you play a card. Volume turns into board damage while you wait for the Trap.

## Filling the exhaust pile on purpose

- **Fan of Knives** — 4 (5) Shivs, all enemies hit. Generate and damage at once.
- **Blade Dance** — add 3 (4) Shivs, Exhaust. The Exhaust is a feature in this build: spent shivs are stored ammunition.
- **Cloak and Dagger** — 6 Block, add 1 (2) Shivs. Every defensive turn still loads the pile.
- **Storm of Steel** (Rare) — discard your hand, add 1 Shiv (upgraded: Shiv+) per card discarded. Turns surplus hands into pile fodder.

## Turn template

1. **Build:** shiv generation plus Accuracy early; spend attacks normally, Expose armored targets.
2. **Scale:** Envenom online; every hit now applies poison — big shiv turns apply most of a boss's death bar on their own.
3. **Cash:** Knife Trap replays the entire exhaust pile upgraded, into Pen Nib if you can line it up — one enemy takes the whole armory, then chokes on the poison it applied.

The hybrid's strength is that it never has a dead reward: shiv cards, poison cards and exhaust payoffs all push the same plan. Its weakness is range — before Knife Trap and Envenom are both online, it is a slower shiv deck, so route defensively in early Act 1 and shop for the two engines.
