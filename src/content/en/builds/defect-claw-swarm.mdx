The **Claw** deck is the Defect's joke that stopped being funny and started deleting bosses: a 0-cost attack that deals 3 (4) damage and permanently raises **every Claw's damage by 2 (3) for the rest of the combat, every time you play any copy**. Cast it fifteen times and each Claw hits for thirty-plus — and the whole deck is built to cast it as often as humanly possible. No orbs required.

## The core loop

- **Claw ×3** (Common, 0) — 3 (4) damage; all Claws gain +2 (3) this combat. Every copy makes every other copy stronger. Take all of them.
- **All for One** (Rare, 2) — deal 10 (14) damage and **put ALL 0-cost cards from your discard pile into your hand**. The retrieval engine: your spent Claws come back for another lap.
- **Hologram** (Common, 1) — 3 (5) Block, put a card from your discard pile into your hand (upgraded: no Exhaust). The second retrieval line — grab All for One back, or another Claw.
- **Overclock** (Uncommon, 0) — draw 2 (3), add a Burn to your discard pile. The filter that finds the pieces; the Burn is a fair tax.
- **Scrape** (Uncommon, 1) — 7 (10) damage, draw 4 (5), discard everything drawn that doesn't cost 0. The deck's perfect cantrip: in a pile of 0-cost cards, it draws four and throws nothing away.

## The 0-cost support line

- **Beam Cell** (Common, 0) — 3 (4) damage, apply 1 (2) Vulnerable. A Claw that also enables +50% damage on everything else.
- **Tempest / Ball Lightning** slots for emergency blocking — but the purist build stays orbless.

## Upgrade priority

1. **Hologram first** — removing its Exhaust is what makes the retrieval loop infinite instead of once-per-fight.
2. **Claw copies** second — base damage speeds every stack.
3. **Overclock** third for draw volume.

## Piloting the swarm

Act 1: collect Claws, Overclock, Scrape; carry a real attack as filler and delete it later. Act 2: the moment All for One and an upgraded Hologram are in the deck, the loop exists — cast Claw until the discard pile fills, All for One pulls every 0-cost card back, Hologram recovers the All for One itself. Act 3: a boss fight is thirty Claw casts deep, each one bigger than the last, with Vulnerable from Beam Cell multiplying the late swings.

The build's discipline: **refuse anything that costs more than 0** (All for One excepted). High-cost cards jam the only mechanic that matters — a thin, fast, all-zero pile. See [deck thinning](/basics/deck-thinning-guide); this deck thins harder than any other in the game.
