The **Particle Wall loop** is the Regent's comfiest engine: a skill that gains Block and **returns itself to your hand**, wrapped in star-producing draw cards, so every turn replays the same defensive cadence while the Stars — and the damage they pay for — climb without limit. It is the least dramatic infinite build in the game until the turn it deals four digits.

## The loop pieces

- **Particle Wall** (Uncommon) — gain 9 (12) Block, **return this card to your Hand**. Permanent defense: once in play, it is a renewable wall that never thins your deck.
- **Glow** (Common) — gain 1 (2) Stars, draw 1, draw 1 next turn. The star faucet on a cantrip.
- **Glimmer** (Uncommon) — draw 3 (4), put 1 card from your hand on top of your draw pile. The volume pump that keeps the wall and the stars cycling.
- **Genesis** (Rare Power) — gain 2 (3) Stars at the start of every turn. Passive income that compounds while the loop runs.
- **Convergence** (Uncommon) — next turn gain energy and Stars, retain your hand. The turn-boundary lubricant.

## The cash-out cards

- **Black Hole** (Uncommon Power) — whenever you spend or gain Stars, deal 3 (4) damage to ALL enemies. In a star loop this is a machine gun: every Glow, every banked turn, every spend ticks the whole board.
- **Radiate** (Uncommon) — deal 3 (4) damage to ALL enemies **for each Star gained this turn**. The burst terminal — the turn you generate ten Stars is the turn Radiate hits for forty.
- **Stardust** (Uncommon) — deal 5 (7) damage to a random enemy X times. The star sink for single-target turns.
- **Seven Stars** (Rare) — 7 damage to ALL enemies, seven times. AOE insurance.

## Building it

The deck wants a short, pure cycle: Particle Wall, every Glow and Glimmer you can carry, Genesis, one Black Hole, one Radiate, and Gather Light (8 (11) Block plus a Star) as a second wall. Delete basic Strikes and Defends without mercy — the loop needs near-100% skill density, and [deck thinning](/basics/deck-thinning-guide) is the whole ritual. Fifteen cards is the dream; twenty is playable.

## The turn pattern

1. **Cycle:** play Glow and Glimmer; draw refills, Stars accrue, Particle Wall replays for Block every rotation.
2. **Bank:** Genesis adds Stars every turn; Royal Gamble (gain 9 Stars, Exhaust) is the emergency reservoir.
3. **Spend:** with a fat Star pile and Black Hole online, cast Radiate — damage scales with Stars gained that turn, and Black Hole adds AOE on every spend besides.
4. **Re-wall:** Particle Wall returns; your Block floor never drops.

The loop's beauty is fail-safety: even a jammed turn still ends with twelve Block on the board. The boss has to win eventually; the Regent's Stars simply decline to stop accumulating first.
