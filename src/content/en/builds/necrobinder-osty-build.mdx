Grow Osty into an unkillable front line — Summon synergies stack health that never wears off.

## The Osty Deck (Summon Tank Archetype)

The Necrobinder starts with a significantly lower HP pool than other characters. The Osty Deck turns that weakness into the build's engine: by using the **Summon** mechanic efficiently you bolster Osty, effectively stacking an endless supply of health — and because Osty's health is not Block, **it doesn't wear off** between turns.

### Core Cards

| Card | Why it earns a slot |
| --- | --- |
| Pull Aggro | Effectively 11 points of Block value, with the upside of Summon synergies. |
| Snap | A cheap Osty attack that enables other cards, with extra value from its Retain. |
| High Five | AoE damage plus Vulnerable is always welcome. |
| Rattle | A key multi-attack source of damage with great combo potential. |
| Fetch | A zero-cost attack that cycles itself — great alongside Rattle. |
| Flatten | Free damage once you have enough Osty attacks in the deck. |
| Necro Mastery | Converts defense into AoE offense — IF you can reliably let Osty grow. |
| Reanimate | A big burst source of Summon; only recommended with spare Energy. |
| Sic 'Em | An Osty attack with great Summon capabilities, particularly alongside Rattle. |
| Spur | Helpful healing once you can consistently keep Osty alive. |

As with the Doom list, the first four are early pickups; Fetch through Spur are the high-synergy payoffs that need commitment.

### Relic to Watch

- **Bone Flute** — it may seem small, but every bit of Block can make all the difference in a build that lives and dies by Osty's survival.

### How to Pilot It

1. The build above focuses on **growing and keeping Osty alive** — protect the hand, and it protects you.
2. There is also a more **sacrificial variant** built around cards like Bone Shards. Both are legitimate; just be careful not to mix and match the wrong cards, because the two plans want different Osty states.
