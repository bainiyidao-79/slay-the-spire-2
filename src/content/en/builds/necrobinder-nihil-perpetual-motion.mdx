The **Ethereal perpetual motion** deck runs on a bargain: Ethereal cards vanish at the end of the turn, but everything in this build pays you for playing them. Sculpting Strike manufactures Ethereal cards on demand, Lethality marks the first attack each turn for +50% (75%), and **Banshee's Cry** — 33 damage to ALL enemies — gets cheaper with every Ethereal cast until it costs nothing at all.

## The engine core

- **Sculpting Strike** (Common, 1) — deal 9 (12) damage and **add Ethereal to a card in your hand**. The converter: any card can join the economy. Take one minimum; it is the build's ignition.
- **Parse** (Uncommon, 1) — Ethereal; draw 3 (4). The cycle card — and once it gains Sly-style discounts or you stack casts, the loop's pump.
- **Pagestorm** (Power, 1) — whenever you draw an Ethereal card, draw 1 more. The multiplier on every draw: one Parse becomes a cascade.
- **Wisp** (Common, 0) — gain energy, Exhaust. Free fuel that never clogs a turn.

## The payoffs

- **Lethality** (Power, 1) — Ethereal; the **first Attack each turn deals 50% (75%) more damage**. Every Ethereal attack you lead with lands enormously.
- **Pull from Below** (Uncommon, 1) — deal 5 (7) damage for **each Ethereal card played this combat**. The scaling clock: the longer the loop runs, the harder this hits.
- **Banshee's Cry** (Rare, 9) — deal 33 damage to ALL enemies, **costing 2 less energy for each Ethereal card played this combat**. Ten Ethereals played turns this into a free board-wipe; that is the deck's endgame.
- **Defile** (Common, 1) — Ethereal; 13 (17) damage. Solid, cheap ammunition that counts toward everything.

## Turn template

1. **Convert:** Sculpting Strike tags a draw card or an attack with Ethereal.
2. **Cycle:** Parse (with Pagestorm online) draws half your deck; Wisp covers the energy.
3. **Lead with Lethality:** the turn's first Ethereal attack swings for half-again.
4. **Cash out:** Pull from Below scales with the combat's Ethereal count; once the counter is deep, Banshee's Cry resolves for 0 and clears the room.

## Deckbuilding cautions

Ethereal cards expire at end of turn, so the deck needs density, not hoarding — 20–25 cards with heavy draw, and the discipline to remove basic Strikes that will never be converted (see [deck thinning](/basics/deck-thinning-guide)). Respect Lethality's target rule: it boosts only the *first* attack, so sequence the big hit before the chip. And keep one real wall — Shroud or Death's Door — because a deck this fast should never need a long fight, but the one turn it does will be against a boss.
