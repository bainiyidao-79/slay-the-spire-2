Slay the Spire 2 launched into Early Access with **five playable classes** — three returning from the original spire-climber and two brand-new characters. Each class bends the deckbuilding formula around a different resource or mechanic, and each one changes how a run feels from the very first combat. Here is every character in the current build, how to unlock them, and what makes them tick.

The unlock chain is strictly linear: each character is freed up by finishing a run with the previous one, so your first-ever run is always an Ironclad run. None of the classes change the base rules of a run — 99 gold to start, three acts to climb, one spire to beat — but every one of them rebuilds your card pool from the ground up.

## Ironclad — the axe that opens the run

The **Ironclad** is the tallest wall in the game at **80 Max HP** and the only character available when you first start. His starter relic, **Burning Blood**, heals 6 HP at the end of every combat, which quietly patches over his favorite strategy: trading health for tempo and healing it back later. Expect big single hits, Vulnerable setup, and cards that want to be exhausted for value.

## Silent — the huntress outside the spire

The **Silent** arrives with **70 Max HP**, the lowest of the returning trio, and compensates with the nastiest toolbox in the game. Her starter relic, **Ring of the Snake**, draws 2 additional cards at the start of each combat. Poison that melts huge health bars, a swarm of low-cost dagger attacks, and discard-synergy draw make her the class of careful, cutthroat sequencing.

## Regent — the heir who lets minions do the work

The **Regent** is the first of the two new characters: **Heir to the Throne of Stars** who wields cosmic powers while his minions do the actual work. He starts at **75 Max HP** with **Divine Right**, gaining three **Stars** — the Regent's unique second energy type — at the start of each combat. Stars pay for cosmic spells and summon work, starting with his signature **Sovereign Blade**.

## Necrobinder — the lich and her left hand

The **Necrobinder** is a Spireborn lich out for revenge, and the squishiest class in the game at **66 Max HP**. Her starter relic, **Bound Phylactery**, Summons 1 at the start of your turn — which in practice means **Osty**, her trusty severed left hand, keeps crawling back into the fight. Keeping Osty alive, Sacrificing him on purpose, and building around undead teamwork is her whole identity.

## Defect — the automaton with orb tech

The **Defect** closes the unlock chain: an automaton eternally modifying itself to survive, at **75 Max HP**. Its starter relic, **Cracked Core**, Channels 1 **Lightning** at the start of each combat. Orb slots, passive charges that trigger at the end of every turn, and elemental evocations make the Defect the most "engine-building" class in the roster — the machine literally deploys orb technology when it must fight.

## One difficulty system for everyone

Every class climbs the same **Ascension** ladder: defeat an Act 3 boss once to unlock Ascension on that character, then keep winning at higher levels to unlock the next one. Modifiers are cumulative, so an Ascension 5 run contains everything Ascension 4 threw at you. Whatever class you main, the spire eventually asks the same question — how far up can you take this deck?
