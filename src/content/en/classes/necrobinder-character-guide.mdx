The **Necrobinder** is a Spireborn lich out for revenge — and Slay the Spire 2's most fragile character at **66 Max HP**. She fights by calling on her trusty left hand, **Osty**, a severed automaton hand that scuttles back into every battle whether she summons him or not. Playing her well means treating Osty less like a pet and more like a resource you are always either investing in or cashing out.

## Necrobinder stats and starter relic

- **Max HP:** 66 — the lowest of all five classes
- **Starting gold:** 99
- **Starter relic:** Bound Phylactery — at the start of your turn, Summon 1
- **How to unlock:** complete a run with the Regent

Bound Phylactery has a quiet rider that defines the class: while you wear it, Osty reappears at the start of every turn with 1 HP if he isn't already on the board. Your left hand simply refuses to stay dead — which is exactly the energy the rest of her kit runs on.

## Osty, the left hand

Osty is the Necrobinder's first and most important **Summon**:

- He occupies a board slot and fights for you, absorbing hits that would otherwise land on her 66 HP.
- Cards in her pool interact with him directly — buffing him, feeding him, or **Sacrificing** him for immediate value.
- Because Bound Phylactery resurrects him every turn, deaths on your side of the board are usually a scheduled part of the game plan, not a disaster.

## How the Necrobinder plays

Her decks divide into three recurring plans:

1. **Osty support** — keep the hand alive and scaling; he becomes a wall that also swings.
2. **Sacrifice engines** — kill Osty (or other summons) on purpose, over and over, converting each death into cards, energy or damage.
3. **Death-adjacent value** — the broader undead toolkit: reanimating, draining, and abilities that care about things dying, enemy or otherwise.

## What a good Necrobinder run looks like

Decide early whether Osty is your bodyguard or your battery. Support builds want health-scaling for the summon and defensive spells for the lich; sacrifice builds want the cheapest possible summon cycle and payoffs that trigger on exit. Her low HP means blocking is never optional — the class that cheats death most is the one that can least afford to experience it.

Build routes we recommend: the [soul-drain engine](/builds/necrobinder-soul-drain), the [ten-thousand-HP hand wall](/builds/necrobinder-ten-thousand-hand-wall), the [calamity burst deck](/builds/necrobinder-calamity-burst) and the [nihil perpetual motion loop](/builds/necrobinder-nihil-perpetual-motion).

## Ascension notes

Her **Ascension** ladder unlocks with an Act 3 boss kill. Ascension modifiers squeeze the narrowest health pool in the game hardest, so high-level Necrobinder runs lean even harder on Osty as a renewable shield.
