## What You Get for $24.99 Right Now

- The full Early Access build: five characters, four-player online co-op, enchantments, branching act variants and Ascension modifiers.
- **Bi-weekly beta patches** — balance changes, new art and UI work ship continuously, and the beta branch graduates to the main branch over time (see [Patch Notes](/news/patch-notes)).
- Steam Deck support out of the box (**Verified**).
