The **Bestiary** shipped in the 0.105 patch as the Compendium's new monster index — and it arrived with both an official secret and a growing pile of community-discovered connections between this spire and the original game's cast. Here is what the book actually does, and what's hiding in it.

## The official secret

Straight from the patch notes: **press the 1–8 keys while viewing a monster** in the Bestiary. The entry responds — a hidden interaction the developers built in for attentive readers. Try it on your favorite horror before you read any further.

## What the Bestiary tracks

The 0.105 version is openly an outline: monster art, animation entries and a listing screen, with full stat blocks and data flagged as still to come. Treat it as the skeleton of a bestiary — the structure ships first, the flesh arrives over Early Access.

## The connections players have traced

The spire's cast carries more continuity than its cartoon monsters let on:

- **Legacy monsters return in cameo forms** — several original-spire creatures reappear as event scenery and fight cameos, complete with their old silhouettes.
- **Enemy mechanics echo the first game's lessons.** Attack patterns veterans remember — big telegraphed singles, multi-hit packs — show up reworked rather than reused.
- **The lore threads run through events more than fights.** The lich's Spireborn origin, the Regent's star throne, and the Ancients' hierarchy are where the sequel's actual story lives; the bestiary entries are the footnotes to those.

## Why the bestiary matters for runs

Even in outline form, it is a scouting tool. Boss entries preview what the end of each act looks like — and knowing the Act 3 boss is the new hourglass construct, [Aeonglass](/mechanics/aeonglass-boss-guide), changes how you draft: hand-pressure answers and block scaling rise in value. The bestiary's real function is letting you prepare for the fights you haven't met yet.

The completionist angle is real too: the index gives the completion-minded a checklist, and the devs have confirmed data, drops and fuller entries will fill in over time. Expect the book to thicken every patch.
