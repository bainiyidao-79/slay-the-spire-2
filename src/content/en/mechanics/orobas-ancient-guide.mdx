**Orobas** — the living rainbow — is one of the possible **Ancients** for the start of Act 2: the blessing-giver who meets you between acts and offers relics that carry no drawbacks, only choices. He is also the most valuable unlock in the late-game rotation, because his pools contain some of the strongest equipment in the spire.

## How to unlock Orobas

Orobas is unlocked by clearing a run with **every character** — all five classes must reach a completed run before he can appear at an Act 2 start. Until then, other Ancients occupy the slot.

## How his shop works

Like every Ancient, Orobas offers **one relic out of each of three pools** — you pick one option per pool rather than a single take-it-or-leave-it gift. That is what makes a fully unlocked Orobas so strong: three separate positive-effect relics, one from each pool.

## The relic pools, highlights verified

**Pool one — deck acceleration:**

- **Glass Eye** — upon pickup, obtain 2 Common, 2 Uncommon and 1 Rare card. The "my deck needs a core" button.
- **Driftwood** — you may reroll each card reward once. The draft-fixer for decks hunting specific pieces.
- **Touch of Orobas** — upon pickup, replace your starter relic with an **Ancient version**. Ironclad's Burning Blood, the Silent's Ring of the Snake and friends all have powered-up Ancient forms.

**Pool two — raw value:**

- **Alchemical Coffer** — upon pickup, gain 4 potion slots **already filled with random potions**. Capacity and inventory in one line.
- **Sea Glass** — see 15 cards from another character; add any number of them to your deck. The cross-class toolkit for fixing a deck's one weakness.

**The upgrade tier:** several of Orobas's relics are upgrade paths for your existing kit — Touch of Orobas's Ancient starter relics (upgraded Burning Blood variants heal more, upgraded orbs Channel harder) turn your class identity up to eleven.

## Choosing between the pools

The classic priorities: if your deck is missing its engine, **Glass Eye**; if your deck is one card away from a loop, **Driftwood**; if your starter relic is your win condition, **Touch of Orobas**. Potion-hungry builds take **Alchemical Coffer** on sight, and Sea Glass is the answer to a deck that keeps losing to one specific problem.
