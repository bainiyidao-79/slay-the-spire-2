**Storm** is a stacking Defect state with one of the cleanest payoffs in the game: *"Whenever you play a Power card, Channel X Lightning"* — where X is the number of Storm stacks you carry. Every Power you cast becomes a Lightning orb; every extra stack makes that fire rate climb.

## How the trigger works

- **Trigger condition:** playing any Power card — rarity and effect don't matter, only the card type.
- **Per-card triggers:** each Power played fires the effect independently; a turn with three Powers means three Storm procs.
- **Orb-slot replacement:** the new Lightning fills the first open slot; when slots are full, the new orb replaces the leftmost orb and triggers its passive on the way out — free extra damage, not waste.
- **Storm is itself a Power:** playing a second Storm first triggers your existing stacks, then adds its own — order matters in the good way.

## What it is worth

Lightning orbs fire at a random enemy whenever their passive triggers, so Storm turns your Power suite into a passive damage engine: every buffs card you play is now also artillery. The pairs naturally:

- **Storm + cheap Powers** — Powers that were already worth playing now generate orbs on top.
- **Storm + orb-slot expansion** — more slots means more Lightning stacking before the replacement cycle starts.
- **Storm + evoke triggers** — effects that evoke orbs convert the accumulated Lightning into burst.

## Where it fits

Storm is the centerpiece of lightning-focused Defect builds — see our [Focus and Lightning build](/builds/defect-focus-lightning) for the full engine around it. The short version: everything the Defect already wanted to play (Powers) now also shoots the enemy.
