**Frail** is the defensive debuff: *"Gain 25% less Block from cards for X turns."* While it is on you, every block number printed on your cards is discounted by a quarter — and the enemies that apply it are timing it for their biggest swing.

## How it works

- **The discount is fixed at 25%** — additional stacks extend the duration by a turn each, they do not deepen the percentage.
- **Rounding is downward:** 5 Block × 25% = 1.25, so you lose 1 and keep 4.
- **Cards only.** Block from relics, potions and other non-card sources is untouched — only the printed numbers on the cards you play get shaved.
- **It expires by turns**, so the debuff's whole cost is concentrated in the window the enemy chose.

## Why enemies love it

The AI applies Frail on the turn before a heavy attack. A 20-Block hand reduced to 15 means five more damage through per swing — and against multi-hit enemies, the loss multiplies across every hit. Frail plus Vulnerable on the same turn is the classic combination that ends unprepared runs.

## Playing around it

1. **Block harder, not smarter:** when Frail is up, simply play more block — the percentage punishes relying on one perfect defensive card.
2. **Non-card block ignores it** — relic and potion defenses are full price; hold them for Frail turns.
3. **Kill the clock:** Frail only lasts X turns. Turtling one extra turn is often cheaper than forcing damage through a debuffed window.
4. **Don't panic-greed:** spending the Frail turns on scaling (Powers, orb setup) instead of taking the damage trade converts the debuff into a wash.

Frail rarely kills by itself; it kills by making an already dangerous turn dangerous-er. See it, count the enemy's next intent, and spend accordingly.
