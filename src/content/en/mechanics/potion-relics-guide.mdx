Potions are free wins stored in glass — and a whole relic economy exists to give you more glass. This guide covers every potion-related relic worth knowing in Slay the Spire 2, sorted by what they actually do: capacity, supply, payoff, and the two relics that pay you to quit potions entirely.

## Capacity: more slots, more options

- **Potion Belt** (Common) — upon pickup, gain 2 potion slots. The baseline expansion; the first pick of any potion-oriented run.
- **Alchemical Coffer** (Ancient, Orobas pool) — gain 4 slots **filled with random potions**. Capacity plus instant inventory; the strongest single potion relic in the game.

## Supply: keep the bottles coming

- **White Beast Statue** (Rare) — potions always appear in combat rewards. Converts every normal fight into a chance at gas, and elites into potion fountains.
- **The Cauldron** (Shop) — upon pickup, brews 5 random potions. A one-time stockpile; best bought right before a hard act.
- **Mailbox-style relics** — rest-site potion drops reward routes that path through campfires; pair with a slot relic so the drops have somewhere to go.

## Payoff: potions that hit back

- **Reptile Trinket** (Uncommon) — whenever you use a potion, gain 3 Strength this turn. The best payoff relic for any deck with attack turns: a healing potion becomes a damage card.
- **Petrified Toad** (Uncommon) — at the start of each combat, procure a Potion-Shaped Rock — a throwable that deals chunk damage to open the fight. Free board-clear tempo, every combat.

## The two heresies: anti-potion relics

- **Buckle-style relics** — while you have no potions, gain Dexterity. A defensive build that never buys potions converts abstinence into block.
- **Energy-for-abstinence relics** — trade the entire potion system for +1 energy per turn. High-cost curve decks that never touched potions anyway lose nothing and gain an energy relic's worth of tempo.

Both are only correct if your deck genuinely ignores potions — taking them "for the stats" into a potion-relic run is burning slots.

## Priority order

1. **Capacity first:** Potion Belt, then Alchemical Coffer. Empty slots are potential; filled slots are wins.
2. **Supply second:** White Beast Statue on combat-heavy routes; The Cauldron as a pre-boss purchase.
3. **Payoff third:** Reptile Trinket on any deck that ends fights with attack damage.
4. **The heresies last,** and only for decks that truly never drink.
