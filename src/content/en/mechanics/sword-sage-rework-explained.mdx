The 0.105 patch quietly rebuilt one of the Regent's best cards. **Sword Sage** (Rare Power, cost 2 (1)) no longer makes the **Sovereign Blade** hit an additional time — instead, it reads: *"Sovereign Blade gains Replay 1."* One word changed, and the whole Forge playbook got faster.

## Old versus new

- **Old Sword Sage:** "Sovereign Blade now hits an additional time." A damage multiplier on each swing — strong, but only when you were already swinging.
- **New Sword Sage:** "Sovereign Blade gains **Replay 1**." Replay is the keyword that repeats a card's effect extra times — and unlike a hit-count boost, it stacks with every other blade enhancer in the kit.

The official patch notes call out the new synergy by name: Sword Sage now combos with **Parry** (the Power that grants the Sovereign Blade bonus Block). Replay multiplies the Parry-boosted swings too — blade damage and blade Block, replayed together.

## Why Replay beats an extra hit

1. **It multiplies Forge scaling.** The Sovereign Blade's damage is Forged up over a combat; a Replay re-triggers the current, fully-scaled value. An extra hit was linear; Replay compounds with every Forge card you've played.
2. **It loves cheap blades.** Apotheosis-style effects that cut the Blade's cost make replayed casts nearly free.
3. **It stacks with the kit.** Seeking Edge (Blade hits ALL enemies), Sword Sage's Replay, and Parry's Block now all layer on the same swing.

## How to use the new Sage

Cast **Sword Sage** before your Forge turn: Forge (or Summon Forth, which Forges 8 (11) on arrival), then swing — the Replay replays the boosted blade immediately. On turns where the Blade is Retained in hand, the sequencing stays the same: Sage first, swing second, and the second swing rides for free.

The rework also nudged the Regent's identity: less "one enormous blade," more "a blade that keeps coming back." Forge decks were already strong; now they end fights before the Block-reliant plans need to.
