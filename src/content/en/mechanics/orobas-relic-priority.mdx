Meeting Orobas with a fully unlocked rotation is a treasure wall: three pools, one pick each, all upside. The right choice depends entirely on what your deck is missing. Here is the priority order that has held up across runs.

<div class="art-ph lead"><img src="/images/sts2-shop.webp" loading="eager" alt="Choosing rewards in Slay the Spire 2"><figcaption>Three pools, no drawbacks, one decision each.</figcaption></div>

## Tier one — take on sight

- **Glass Eye** — obtain 2 Common, 2 Uncommon and 1 Rare card on pickup. If your deck hasn't found its engine, this is the single fastest fix in the game: five cards of targeted density, no deck slots wasted on maybes.
- **Radiant Pearl** — at the start of each combat, add 1 **Luminesce** (the 0-cost, 2-energy spell) to your hand. Free opening-burst every fight, forever; energy-starved decks — the Silent especially — climb a tier the moment this lands.
- **Driftwood** — reroll each card reward once. For decks hunting a specific rare, this converts "maybe" rewards into a search bar, fight after fight.

## Tier two — build-dependent

- **Touch of Orobas** — replaces your starter relic with its Ancient version. Spectacular on classes whose starter relic IS the build (Ironclad's healing, the Necrobinder's summon engine); merely nice elsewhere.
- **Alchemical Coffer** — 4 potion slots, pre-filled. Potion-centric plans and elite-rush routes get real value; passive decks can pass.
- **Sea Glass** — view 15 cards from another character and add any number. The patch-a-hole pick: one targeted cross-class card fixes many decks' fatal matchup.

## The decision tree

1. **Deck has no engine yet?** Glass Eye.
2. **Engine works but the deck runs out of gas?** Radiant Pearl.
3. **Deck is one card from done?** Driftwood.
4. **Starter relic is central to the plan?** Touch of Orobas.
5. **Elite-heavy route planned?** Alchemical Coffer.
6. **One specific card would fix everything?** Sea Glass.

## A note on timing

Orobas appears at the start of **Act 2** — the moment your deck's shape is knowable. Spend the last few fights of Act 1 asking the only question that matters for this choice: *what exactly is my deck missing?* The pool that answers that question is the pool to pick.
