The 0.105 patch hit the Silent's pet card: **Blade of Ink's** Inky enchantment damage bonus dropped from **+2 to +1**. The card still does its job — it just pays out a little slower. Here is what changed, what didn't, and how the Inky plan rebuilds.

<div class="art-ph lead"><img src="/images/sts2-char-silent.webp" loading="eager" alt="The Silent character art in Slay the Spire 2"><figcaption>One less damage per knife. The knives didn't notice.</figcaption></div>

## The exact change

Blade of Ink (Rare Skill): "Add 2 (3) Inky Shivs into your Hand." The Inky enchantment on those shivs carried a +2 damage bonus before 0.105; now it carries **+1**. Per copy, per shiv, per cast — a small trim repeated across a long fight.

## What the nerf does NOT change

- **The shiv count.** Blade of Ink still manufactures 2 (3) knives per cast — volume is untouched.
- **The synergies.** Every multiplier that made Inky shivs strong still applies: **Accuracy** (+4 (6) per shiv), **Fan of Knives** (shivs hit ALL enemies), the Boot's 5-damage floor, Pen Nib's every-10th-attack double, and Finisher's per-attack tally. The Inky bonus was one layer of several.
- **The exhaust-pile plan.** If you are feeding **Knife Trap**, shivs care about existing, not their printed damage — the replay plays them regardless.

## The rebuild

The post-nerf Inky plan simply shifts weight from the enchantment to the classic engine:

1. **Accuracy is mandatory again.** At +4 (6), it out-earnest the nerfed Inky bonus from turn one; two copies is normal, three is not greed.
2. **Play the volume game.** Blade Dance, Fan of Knives and Up My Sleeve produce more knives per turn than one point of Inky damage ever added.
3. **Keep the payoffs.** Finisher, Pen Nib and Knife Trap all count shivs, not shiv damage — the nerf never touched them.

## The honest verdict

Before 0.105, Blade of Ink was quietly a rare-tier auto-pick: production plus a damage enchant in one card. After it, the card is a very good shiv generator with a bonus — still worth taking in any knife deck, no longer the reason the deck wins. The Inky plan survives; it just needs its engine back around it.
