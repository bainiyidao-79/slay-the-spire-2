The 0.105 beta patch is a structural one: Mega Crit shifts to bi-weekly updates, ships the game's first Bestiary, replaces an Act 3 boss entirely, and rebalances cards across every class. Here is everything that matters, verified against the official patch notes.

<div class="art-ph lead"><img src="/images/sts2-slide-classes.webp" loading="eager" alt="Slay the Spire 2 key art"><figcaption>A patch that changes the rhythm of the whole Early Access.</figcaption></div>

## The cadence change

Updates move from weekly to **every two weeks**. The official reasoning: weekly patches were too much work, and the slower rhythm buys time for bigger changes and for beta players to actually absorb each patch.

## The Bestiary arrives

The Compendium now includes the **Bestiary** — an outline of the game's monsters with their art and animation entries (full data still coming). And it ships with a secret, straight from the patch notes: **press the 1–8 keys while viewing a monster.**

## Doormaker out, Aeonglass in

Act 3's **Doormaker** has been replaced by a brand-new boss, **Aeonglass**. The official line: Doormaker's fight was over the complexity threshold and carried lingering issues, so the team rebuilt the slot from scratch. Our [Aeonglass boss guide](/mechanics/aeonglass-boss-guide) covers the new fight.

## Class balance highlights

**Silent** — **Blade of Ink** nerfed: the Inky enchantment's damage bonus drops from +2 to +1.

**Regent** — **Sword Sage** reworked: no longer makes the Sovereign Blade hit an extra time; now the Sovereign Blade **gains Replay 1** — which newly synergizes with Parry. Bulwark's Block drops to 12 (15); **Crescent Spear** buffed from 6 to 8 base damage; Patter's Block gain trimmed to 8 (10); **Royalties** gold raised to 30 (40).

**Defect** — **Infused Core** relic now also makes Lightning orbs deal 1 additional damage; **Hyperbeam** up to 28 (36); **Shatter** now Evokes all of your orbs twice; **Tesla Coil+** reshaped — damage down from 6 to 4, but Lightning triggers doubled from once to twice; **Uproar** up to 6 (8).

**Colorless** — **Gold Axe** in multiplayer now counts cards played by ALL players, not just its owner.

## Enemy changes

- **Fossil Stalker:** gains a set amount of Strength when hitting any player, instead of per-player stacking.
- **Haunted Ship:** applies 3 Weak on turn 1, instead of on attack.
- **Soul Fysh:** Scream damage raised from 11 (12) to 13 (15).

## Ancients relics

- **Tezcatara's Pumpkin Candle** reworked: 1 energy per turn, now extinguishes **after 5 combats** (not at Act 3), and **can be re-Kindled at rest sites**.
- Three new Neow relics: **Kaleidoscope** (temp name; gain two card rewards from other characters), **Fishing Rod** (every 3 normal combats, upgrade a random card), **Silken Tress** (enchant the first card reward with Glam).
- **Tezcatara's Seal of Gold** moves to reward option 3, sharing Pumpkin Candle's pool.

## The stealth star: Lantern Keys

One line in the events section: *"Something special now happens if you have multiple Lantern Key cards"* — single-player only. Our [multi-Lantern Key guide](/basics/multiple-lantern-keys-secret) explains the payoff.
