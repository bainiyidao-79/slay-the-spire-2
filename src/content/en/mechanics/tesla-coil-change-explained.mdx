The 0.105 patch rebalanced **Tesla Coil+**, the Defect's free Lightning-trigger, in the least obvious way possible: it made the card worse on paper and dramatically better in practice. Damage dropped from 6 to 4 — but the Lightning trigger count **doubled from once to twice**.

<div class="art-ph lead"><img src="/images/sts2-char-defect.webp" loading="eager" alt="The Defect with a rack of Lightning orbs in Slay the Spire 2"><figcaption>Two triggers beat one bonus point. It's not close.</figcaption></div>

## The exact change

- **Tesla Coil (unupgraded):** 0 cost — deal 3 (4) damage, trigger all your Lightning orbs once.
- **Tesla Coil+ (0.105):** 0 cost — deal 4 damage, **trigger all your Lightning orbs twice**.

The card is still 0-cost with no Exhaust. It still fires every Lightning orb you have. The only thing that changed is the ratio between its pocket change and its real effect.

## The math on why doubling wins

Each Lightning trigger deals each orb's passive damage. With **one** Lightning orb, the old card's extra damage roughly cancelled the new card's loss. With **two or more** orbs, the second trigger outearns the 2-point cut immediately — and the gap widens linearly with every orb in the rack:

- **3 Lightning orbs:** the new card triggers six passive hits per cast instead of three.
- **5–6 orbs (late-game racks):** the second trigger is worth double-digit damage per cast, repeated every turn, for free.

Add Focus scaling — each orb's passive grows with Focus, so every trigger inherits the multiplier — and the doubled trigger count scales exponentially while the lost 2 damage stays exactly 2.

## How to build around it

- **Fill slots, don't over-invest in one orb:** the value is per-trigger, so quantity beats quality. **Capacitor** (2 (3) extra slots) and cheap Lightning channels are the partners.
- **Focus still stacks on top:** Defragment and Biased Cognition raise every trigger's damage.
- **Cast it every single turn:** it's free. The only wrong way to use Tesla Coil+ is forgetting to.

The rebalance is a quiet thesis statement about the class: the Defect's power was never one orb — it was the rack. The patch just made the rack louder.
