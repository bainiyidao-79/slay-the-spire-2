Some relics win runs. The **loyalty badge** — the thank-you token from the game's shopper-event chain — exists to be looked at. Its entire rule text is: **no effect.** No buff, no curse, no interaction with your deck, your energy, shop prices or combat math. It is a commemorative trinket, full stop.

## The facts

- **Type:** general relic — any character
- **Rarity:** Event
- **Source:** the shopper-appreciation event reward
- **Effect:** none — it provides no bonus, triggers nothing, and carries no penalty. It does not touch your deck, energy, draw, combat resolution or shop prices in any way.

## Does a dead slot matter?

Relics are mostly passive inventory — there is no "relic slot limit" to worry about — so a no-effect relic costs you nothing except the event's alternative reward. The only real question is the opportunity cost at the moment of pickup:

- **If the choice is badge vs. nothing**, take it — a relic that does nothing is still a relic, and some future card, event or achievement may care that you own one.
- **If the choice is badge vs. a real relic or resources**, the comparison is lopsided: take the functional reward.

## Why it exists

Flavor. The game is full of deadpan items that exist for world-building and the occasional achievement checklist, and the badge is the purest example: proof that somewhere in the spire, customer loyalty is honored, right before the customer is murdered by skeletons.

The practical guide: pick it up if it is free, skip it if it competes, and never build around it — there is nothing to build around.
