**The Boot** is the game's smallest hammer and its most reliable: *"Whenever you would deal 4 or less unblocked attack damage, increase it to 5."* Every tiny hit in your deck now lands for a flat 5 — and in a shiv deck, "every tiny hit" adds up to thousands.

## The facts

- **Type:** general relic — any character can use it
- **Rarity:** Event
- **Source:** the **Trash Heap** question-mark event's rummage option — not boss rewards, not chests
- **Effect:** whenever you would deal 4 or less **unblocked attack damage**, raise it to 5

## The fine print

- **Attack damage only.** Poison ticks, burn, thorns and self-damage never trigger it — and negative or zero damage doesn't count either.
- **"Unblocked" means unblocked.** If the enemy's Block eats the entire hit — 4 damage into 4 Block — no damage got through, and the Boot has nothing to raise.
- **It resolves last.** The raise happens after enemy damage-reduction and immunity effects ( dodge-style intangible states and the like), lifting a reduced chip into a clean 5.
- **It doesn't stack:** the floor is 5, not 5-per-Boot.

## Who wants it

- **Shiv and multi-hit decks** — the Silent's swarm and the Claw engine turn "raise every small hit to 5" into a flat damage tax on every cast. Ten small hits gain twenty-plus damage per turn.
- **Weak-affected turns** — when your big attack is halved by an enemy Weak, small hits at the Boot's floor often outdamage it.
- **Everyone else** — it is a quiet, free upgrade to any deck that sneezes small damage, with zero deckbuilding cost.

The honest review: the Boot is never a run-defining relic and never a dead one. It is five damage, forever, in the pile where five damage was missing.
